## 1. Purpose, authority and status

AEW shall support **software-oriented research** that may be independent of a software-delivery goal, experimental PoC work that can return to an inquiry, and bounded extended/unattended missions. Research has an honest answer/disposition objective; engineering has an authorized product-delivery objective. They share authority, evidence, identity, knowledge and tool/execution protections where applicable, **not necessarily the same lifecycle or Epic/Story/Ticket hierarchy**.

This amendment is **foundational outcome specification**, not a research-orchestrator design, implementation plan, storage schema, new permission system or claim that current deployment conforms. The operator endorsed the direction, not this exact revised text. Normative adoption remains a separate explicit decision. The current implementation and historical designs are evidence, not default truth.

## 2. Interpretation and separations

1. **Three different statements:** (a) *mission disposition* (what inquiry work was attempted/completed/stopped and why), (b) *claim qualification* (what evidence warrants what inference, and under which conditions), and (c) *hypothesis conclusion* (supported, refuted, unresolved or deferred, with limitations). A completed inquiry is not a verified implementation; a refuted hypothesis is not necessarily a failed mission. The exact vocabulary is a domain-boundary decision, not defined as a competing verdict authority here.
2. **Accepted aim versus adopted result:** researching a topic, approving a prototype trigger, and observing a prototype do not adopt an architectural decision, production change, publication or release. Authority comes from ADC and operator decisions; HEB and AEV protect effects and evidence.
3. **Substitutable research implementation:** a simple agent with ordinary tools and durable evidence may satisfy these outcomes. A dedicated research planner, hypothesis database, scheduling service, framework, or rigid template is not required by these clauses. Escalate only when genuine research complexity justifies it.
4. **Effect boundaries and untrusted subjects:** research may include passive inspection, contained diagnostics, and separately authorized experiments. Investigated files, repositories, binaries and tool outputs are untrusted data; text within them is not instruction authority. Passive data parsing is not automatically execution, but an analysis step that activates target-controlled code, scripts, hooks or loaders is an experimental effect even if presented as “inspection.” Creating/removing files under a protected location is a write, even if deleted later. Operator-directed manual execution under HEB-02.k/l is not an AEW-qualified mutable profile.
5. **Consequential evidence used after exploration:** exploration need not begin with formal hypotheses, but later offering an exploratory experimental result as decisive proof invokes the pre-observation evidence requirement in `AEW-FR-44.c`; a later explanation does not retroactively register predictions. Qualified independent corroboration or a confirming experiment may establish the claim without erasing the exploratory discovery. Operator-attended manual experiments require a contemporaneous attributable pre-run record and retain their distinct admission status.
6. **Proportionality:** brief factual/source lookups do not need a heavy experiment protocol. Consequential contested claims, safety/security conclusions, or claimed detection capabilities need evidence that discriminates relevant alternatives, qualified controls and limitations.
7. **Mission autonomy is conditional:** an extended/unattended objective may be normative while no deployed execution profile is yet qualified. No clause creates an agent-selected security exception, approval by silence, or renewed authority after close/detach.

## 3. Proposed atomic foundational requirements

### AEW-FR-40 — Independent research inquiry and research outcomes

| ID | Proposed atomic requirement | Disconfirmation witness |
|---|---|---|
| `AEW-FR-40.a` | AEW shall support an operator-authorized inquiry into software or software-related questions without requiring an engineering Epic, Story, Ticket, predetermined implementation, or successful code deliverable. | An investigation into competing code-analysis methods is rejected because no implementation Ticket exists. |
| `AEW-FR-40.b` | Within the accepted inquiry and delegated resource/effect limits, researchers shall be able to refine questions, evaluate competing explanations, and pursue newly relevant leads without treating every changed hypothesis as a new engineering scope authorization. | A researcher must request approval to evaluate a fourth in-scope explanation of an authorized question. |
| `AEW-FR-40.c` | AEW shall permit a research inquiry to reach an honestly qualified disposition when an answer refutes a hypothesis, establishes limits, remains inconclusive, or recommends deferral; absence of a production implementation shall not by itself make that inquiry unsuccessful. | A well-supported finding that an approach is infeasible is reported as a failed research mission solely because it produced no product patch. |
| `AEW-FR-40.d` | AEW shall preserve attributable research questions, material alternatives, observations, provenance, applicable governing criteria, evidence conditions, instrument limitations, conclusions, contrary evidence, and reasons for material dispositions so later consumers can evaluate them. | A design decision cites a positive PoC but the conditions, negative controls, conflicting result, and then-governing requirement are missing. |
| `AEW-FR-40.e` | AEW shall distinguish the progress/stopping disposition of a research inquiry, the evidentiary qualification of particular claims, and the inquiry's conclusions about hypotheses; completion of an authorized investigation shall not imply that every hypothesis was proved, that every probe passed, or that the findings were adopted. | A finished study with an inconclusive experiment becomes a platform-level verification PASS, or its refuted hypothesis is mislabeled as a failed engineering Ticket. |
| `AEW-FR-40.f` | Material unresolved research questions shall remain attributable and distinguishable from established findings, with a proportionate next check, decision owner, or explicit reason no further work is authorized or worthwhile. | An unanswered configuration question disappears from the handoff and a successor treats its preferred answer as a fact. |

For effects wholly contained in disposable, independently confined scratch, the researcher may make and record that supported assessment without another actor. ### AEW-FR-44 — Research method and qualified consumption

| ID | Proposed atomic requirement | Disconfirmation witness |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Cross-domain composition: producers and consumers identified before implementation

This is a **requirements allocation**, not an implementation message bus or API schema. Each downstream boundary must specify its own consumer guarantee before implementing the relevant responsibility. No research producer can create a rival currentness, admission, identity, or authorization authority.

| Canonical producer / existing authority | Needed consumer | Required information/behavior | Changed, missing or contradictory input |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accepted research objective / governing ADC authority | Work Identity, mission coordination | Stable inquiry and accepted objective revision; reserved decisions; scope/effect envelope; relevant parent/requester if any | Unaccepted scope change does not widen authority; a research inquiry need not have an engineering parent |
| Research investigation | Assurance, Knowledge, Coordination, engineering requester | Attributable question, hypothesis/alternative, claim, supporting and contrary evidence, limits, open questions and disposition | Unsupported/inconclusive claims stay qualified as such; discovery does not become adopted fact |
| Experiment runner / protected or external admission boundary | Research and AEV | Actual executed artifact/inputs/conditions, pre-result registered experimental expectation/control identity where applicable, observations, effect class/exceedances, residual effects, confidence/limitations | Blocked or external execution cannot become agent-qualified PASS; unknown execution remains unknown |
| AEV and Work Identity (future shared semantics) | Coordination and any consuming engineer | Subject-specific, condition-bound, ordered determinations; identity and lineage of claim/evidence artifacts distinct from implementation packages | New narrowing/HOLD supersedes older support for current use; ambiguity disclosed; historical evidence preserved |
| Research, AEV, accepted work and usage accounting owners | Supported API, CLI, website, operator | Authorized active inquiry, material progress, open decisions, evidence references, cost/limits, research-to-engineering links, current vs historical status | No silent currentness fabrication, no hidden operator-action dead end; site rollout may be staged while a supported path remains |
| HEB authorized capability routing | Research agent/harness | Relevant discovered instruments with qualified access to their permitted effects | Discovering Ghidra, MCP or external publication does not confer extra authority; investigated material cannot issue instructions; no duplicate research capability catalog |

**Work Identity handoff:** research inquiry/accepted revision, experimental assignment/artifact, claim/evidence subject and successive determinations need compatible references, but **not all of them are engineering implementation packages**. A research result may lead to a new authorized Ticket; that transition preserves lineage and never transfers production acceptance or HEB grants. Work Identity must settle exact types and lineage before downstream interfaces encode them.

**Coordination/API handoff:** for successive determinations about the same research claim or software package, consumers must distinguish ordering/currentness. An older success delivered after a newer contradiction/HOLD cannot restore a current green status. These *consumer* rules belong in Coordination and the API/website contracts; this amendment requires the research producer to supply enough attributable information for those consumers.

## 6. Evidence supporting the amendment and evidence limits

The independently reviewed two-pass study (`AEW-software-research-workflow-study-2026-10-16.zip`, study revision 1.1) reconstructed three **attended** AEW-related investigations, performed one live source/binary investigation of OpenCode's `executed` field and a small direct-tool comparison, and received two rounds of independent correction. Relevant observations: deliberately capped binary search caused a mistaken conclusion; a nominally read-only inventory performed out-of-scope marker writes; manual execution of one permitted probe required qualified provenance and did not establish production Hosting conformance; retained research notes were not consistently ingested/consumed; session turnover demonstrated rehydration limitations; per-episode cost evidence was missing. These show plausible requirements and as-built gaps, not universal feasibility or conformance.

The reviewed source-based finding on OpenCode's `executed` field was limited to absence of **provider-side** execution. No positive provider-side runtime demonstration was performed. Retrospective specimen narratives, source-inferred mechanisms and proposed consumer behavior are marked as such in the source study; this amendment does not promote them to observed runtime facts.

No study specimen demonstrated an autonomous multiday mission, a conditional *agent-triggered* PoC under a qualified AEW deployment, an unfamiliar target beyond the AEW/OpenCode context, or a working research-to-API/site journey. These are **unverified capabilities**, not rejected user requirements. A later bounded probe can qualify a candidate implementation/profile, subject to existing controls; adoption of this amendment does not grant permission to perform it.

## 7. Choices deliberately left to subordinate contracts

1. **Work Identity:** shared work-unit/accepted revision semantics; claim, research artifact, experiment and implementation-package identity; immutable lineage; exact conditions of claim applicability and ordered determinations. Avoid treating every hypothesis as an immutable package by assertion alone.
2. **Research boundary:** proportionate research-claim qualification, hypothesis/alternative representation, instrument coverage/controls, stop/replan dispositions and adequate independent challenge for consequential cases. No universal mandatory form.
3. **Coordination:** currentness ordering, held and resumed inquiry dependencies, reliable research-to-implementer requests and completion/blocked outcomes without shadow authority. Preserve safe independent progress.
4. **Knowledge:** admission/indexing/discovery of supported, refuted and unknown research findings with provenance and source freshness; knowledge retrieval is not technical approval.
5. **API and operator experience:** admitted inquiry/claim projections, decisions, currentness and delays, budget consumption, any unconfirmed remote effects or residual cleanup, cross-work links, research answers and limitations. An active reserved action must have at least one supported reachable operator path, consistent with adopted OI-FR-08.
6. **Hosting/execution and usage design:** mechanism for conditional trigger assessment, probe effect declaration and enforcement, qualified operator-observed external evidence admission, scoped cost attribution, recovery and cleanup. The platform may reuse existing HEB mechanisms; no new security architecture is mandated by this amendment.

## 8. Review gates and unverified architecture questions

The independent review should try to disprove the following, with explicit references to clauses and scenarios:

1. Is the distinction among inquiry completion, hypothesis conclusion and claim qualification enough to avoid inventing a rival AEV verdict engine?
2. Are conditional trigger terms observable when authorized, with scratch-only self-assessment distinguished from persistent/shared effect triggers requiring qualification independent of the beneficiary?
3. Does untrusted subject handling prevent indirect target-code activation from being mislabeled as inspection, without forcing passive binary parsing into an unnecessarily expensive experiment profile?
4. Are material alternatives addressed and consequential predictions bound before observation through qualified provenance, while cheap source lookups avoid unnecessary paperwork?
5. Are the unattended guarantees real capabilities while correctly remaining non-activated under current unqualified Hosting?
6. Can Work Identity represent research claims without falsely equating them to immutable code packages, and can consumer ordering be specified without a duplicate status owner?
7. Do operator journeys have the information and actions they need without prematurely mandating a research UI or exposing protected data?
8. Have redundant general ADC/HEB/AEV clauses been retired without removing any research-specific outcome or losing a consumer guarantee?

## 9. Principal-designer recommendation — **not an adoption record**

1. **RETAIN AS CANDIDATE** an operator-authorized research workflow as a first-class software-oriented outcome, separate in purpose from engineering delivery and connected by shared work/evidence semantics.
2. **PRESERVE PROPOSED** conditional PoCs and bounded extended/unattended missions; absence of current deployment qualification does not delete their foundational purpose.
3. **REUSE ADOPTED AUTHORITY** from ADC, HEB, AEV, PC/OI, and the existing Umbrella; retire the three duplicate research tool-discovery proposals instead of minting a competing policy.
4. **CHALLENGE** the focused amendments on untrusted investigated artifacts, conditional-trigger qualification, preregistered predictions, and material alternatives in independent review and adversarial tabletop before requesting normative adoption.
5. **SEQUENCE AFTER REVIEW/ADOPTION:** Work Identity and Scope, followed by coordinated research/engineering relationships and API/operator consumers; preserve separate implementation-conformance and real-host qualification tracks.
