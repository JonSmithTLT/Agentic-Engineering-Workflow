**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Document role:** Implementation-independent domain/boundary contract for applicable assurance, qualified evidence, independent verification, correction, and completion conclusions.  
**Design basis:** Operator elicitation, two v0.1 and two v0.2 independent-review reports, v0.3 focused independent review, and composition-by-design direction (endorsed, amendment not yet adopted), checked against the operator-adopted Foundational Requirements v0.4, Engineering Workflow Umbrella v0.4, Authority/Delegation/Classification v0.3.1, and Hosting/Execution Boundaries v0.3.1.  
**Evidence status:** Contract drafting and example scenarios only. **No architectural scenario in this document has been executed as a conformance test; no AEW implementation is certified by this document.**

> **Contract intent:** Be strict about the truth of assurance claims and about protected authority; be economical about how legitimate engineering work reaches those claims. A failed check should normally yield a targeted, understandable correction path—not loss of valid work, an unauthorized waiver, or an incentive to avoid verification.

## 1. Boundary, precedence, and interpretation

### 1.1 Authority and scope

This candidate specifies how AEW determines what assurance is required, receives and qualifies evidence, preserves findings, handles corrections and disputes, and establishes technical completion. It refines—not replaces—the four already-adopted baselines. A later candidate, review, implementation habit, or test-result convention **does not** authorize changes to accepted requirements, mandatory checks, scope, delegation, release policy, or safety controls.

The adoption status of the source files must be read through their separate operator adoption records; historical candidate headers in byte-pinned adopted sources are not current authority notices. Source identifiers are recorded in §8. The proposed Research/Experimentation/Unattended amendment is **not adopted** and is not made normative here.

Where this candidate uses **shall**, the sentence states a proposed required outcome *if adopted and applicable*; **may** identifies an authorized option. A negative/violation witness illustrates falsifiability but is not a full test plan. The identifiers `AEV-NN.x` are proposed stable atomic contract IDs. The scenarios `AEV-Xnn` are non-normative architectural challenges.

This contract does not establish a second authoritative Ticket store, requirements registry, approval system, defect database, agent scheduler, test-runner product, CI layout, or new grant mechanism. The existing responsible authority owns requirements and any changes to them; ADC governs grants, eligibility, classification and adjudicator selection; HEB governs protected execution. Assurance determines **what evidence supports which claim**.

### 1.2 Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Important exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Assurance obligation** | An applicable governing check, assessment, criterion, or evidence duty whose satisfaction is necessary for the specified claim | Not a verifier's free-floating preference |
| **Governing verification basis** | The applicable protected requirements, mandatory checks, acceptance criteria, thresholds, exclusions, and qualified identities of effective executable-check definitions when admitted, including authorized later revisions | Not every test file in the repository; an assignment may require a check whose implementation does not yet exist |
| **Verification subject / package** | An identifiable immutable implementation/integration state, relevant inputs/dependencies, and acceptance scope against which a claim is evaluated | Not a generic Ticket label or all future commits |
| **Observation** | What a tool, reviewer, test, or operator actually observed under identified conditions | Not automatically a conclusion about correctness |
| **Qualified evidence** | An attributable observation or assessment whose source, subject, relevance, limitations and integrity are adequate for its claimed use | Not merely an exit code or authored assertion |
| **Assurance disposition** | The applicable obligation-level conclusion: satisfied, violated, insufficient, blocked, or otherwise explicitly qualified | Not necessarily a new Ticket lifecycle state |
| **Finding** | An attributable defect, evidence gap, discrepancy, or optional improvement with stated affected criteria and rationale | Not automatically a new requirement or blocking gate |
| **Material assurance dependency** | A behavior/interface/environment reliance whose unresolved validity could change the truth of a claimed obligation | Not every source-code import |
| **Independent assessor** | An actor/function eligible under the adopted ADC independence and lineage rules for the *specific* assessment | Not an assessor selected freely by the assessed Lead |

### 1.3 Interpretation and default behavior

The level of assurance derives from governing policy, material risk, claimed capability, affected boundaries and qualified evidence—not line count, number of tests, agent confidence, or a desire to reduce CI time. The required assurance policy/thresholds are supplied by an authorized policy owner, not improvised by the verifier or implementer. Where a necessary assurance applicability or qualification rule is unknown, the affected mandatory claim remains held pending authorized resolution; unrelated authorized work need not stop.

An accepted assignment shall establish its governing verification obligations when admitted, derived from the authority-confirmed acceptance criteria and applicable rule-computed ADC control floor rather than implementer-selected minimum checks. When a required executable check is not yet implemented, its obligation is recorded as unresolved; the later check definition must be qualified and associated with the basis before it can satisfy that obligation. Newly discovered applicable mandatory obligations must be brought into the basis when established by governing authority; routine supplementary checks may be added. Removal or weakening is controlled and cannot be tuned retrospectively to the test outcome. A partial observation may support a scoped positive conclusion. It does not automatically yield Ticket completion, scope acceptance, integration readiness, publication permission or waiver of an unresolved mandatory criterion. A new revision can preserve useful work and old evidence without inheriting an unqualified PASS. All ordinary work and diagnostics remain subject to current grants and HEB execution boundaries.

## 3. Evidence and status interface — required information, not a new schema

The canonical work/authority systems may already store these facts. A conforming implementation can reference existing immutable files, test receipts, work records, evaluator outputs or other durable artifacts; no additional database or universal JSON format is required.

For an assurance claim, an independent consumer must be able to recover:

1. **Claim and authority:** the assignment-admission verification basis and its authorized subsequent revisions; the governing objective, specifically applicable `AEW-FR`/`EW-U`/domain obligations, current authorized criteria, mandatory vs optional status, responsible deciding role, and whether the relevant requirement is currently due/applicable.
2. **Subject:** immutable Ticket/work identity and revision (where defined), concrete code/integration state, material build/runtime dependencies and applicable environment/profile identity; exact identity/binding mechanisms belong to the Work Identity and Hosting designs.
3. **Evidence:** observation producer and independently established observation provenance or execution receipt when mandatory, eligible independence if claimed, check/fixture/inputs, environment assumptions, actual results including retained negatives, qualified redactions, trust limits, and whether findings are confirmed or merely alleged.
4. **Reasoning:** accepted applicability and sufficiency decisions, carry-forward links to original evidence, material changes, contradictions and how resolved, required checks not executed, deferrals, waivers or quarantine authorized by the proper authority.
5. **Disposition:** per-obligation outcome and its limitations; exact package-level verification determination; held dependent claims; and unresolved non-blocking findings plus the Lead's disposition.

**Claim vocabulary (logical, not mandated storage enums):** A governing obligation can be *satisfied for stated conditions*, *violated*, *insufficient evidence*, *blocked/unexecuted*, or *not applicable with authority-grounded rationale*. Evidence can also be *historical/stale for current use* or *contradicted*. These dimensions must not be collapsed into a single green/red flag. A completed workflow stage is not itself proof of behavior. **No percentage of tests passed is a correctness percentage.**

**Package binding:** The verified subject should be precise enough to prevent passing an untested revision, but narrowly enough to avoid coupling every independent work item to unrelated repository changes. Immutable identities, selective group digests and revision semantics shall align with the eventual Work Identity contract; this candidate does not mandate hashing the entire repository or a particular E-series implementation. For source changes or changed dependency effects, prior evidence remains original evidence and a **new, separately qualified applicability judgment** is needed before using it to support the new package.

**Evidence trust boundary:** Agent self-check logs remain useful for local iteration but are not independently trusted mandatory-check results. A protected/independently observed run or verifiable receipt must establish relevant subject and actual outcome; trusted observation of command exit alone does not establish test adequacy or source integrity. For mandatory enumerated executable checks, the evidence must reconcile governing identity/version with what was actually selected, collected, executed, skipped or omitted, and the observed outcome. Trusting a runner's exit status does not automatically make agent-controlled test selection or a test-process-produced report trustworthy. Operator-observed manual evidence is explicitly qualified to the observation and does not silently prove HEB profile conformance. Material redactions must be attributable.

**Security boundary:** Reviewer-written probes and tests are untrusted agent-authored material. Protected verifiers and admission components do not obtain permission to run them under privileged identities merely because they are named “tests” (HEB C6). Operator-attended external/manual contributions remain outside AEW-protected execution and require ordinary admission. Credentials and sensitive evidence must follow the adopted source/secret and effect boundaries.


### 3.1 Contractual producer–consumer composition (candidate allocation)

The following describes **necessary domain relationships before implementation**, not a promise that current code or API routes already implement them. Successive determinations for one package can differ, so consumers must not rely on package identity alone to decide whether a PASS or subsequent contradiction is current. The pending Product Cohesion/Operator Experience foundational amendment is not yet normative; these are Assurance's proposed duties under the currently adopted cross-component and operator-reporting requirements. Each interface needs an accountable owner and consumer-specific guarantees.

| Producer / authoritative decision | Consumer and required use | Contractual boundary and failure behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted assignment criteria, classification and ADC control floor | Assurance | Receive the admitted verification basis and authorized changes; unresolved mandatory parameters hold only affected claims. Assurance cannot lower the floor. |
| Assurance's package-bound outcomes, evidence gaps and holds | Coordination, integration and completion functions | Consume qualified assurance scoped to the exact package/obligation; prevent dependent completion based on absent or contradicted claims while permitting safe independent activity. | Assurance's findings, diagnostic evidence, qualified results and later contradictions | Work-history and knowledge/evidence admission consumers | Preserve provenance, confidentiality, source trust and relevance; no conversion of raw agent reports into qualified facts merely because they are stored or retrieved. An eventual API contract must allocate which of these facts are intentionally exposed and which are protected or unavailable. The interface need not expose every raw log, but it must not mask material qualification, current versus historical status, or unresolved blocking outcomes in supported operator journeys.

## 4. Normal assurance journey and exception disposition

The following is an explanatory workflow, **not** a new mandated per-Ticket state machine or fixed role count:

1. **Derive and record obligations at assignment admission** from authority-confirmed scope and the computed assurance/classification floor—not from a developer-selected minimal checklist—then preserve attributable supplements, newly applicable obligations and authorized weakening/removal. A routine Ticket may use cheap self-checks if allowed; a consequential boundary receives its mandatory independent and behavioral evidence.
2. **Implement and gather evidence** within the assignment. Use targeted and relevant existing tests; do not generate gratuitous new regression executables. Keep observations bound to the tested state.
3. **Review and verify** with the applicable independence. For governing executable checks, qualify the effective check definition (including behavior-altering fixtures/hooks) and reconcile its observed identity, collection and execution against the recorded basis, not just check labels or command exit. The verifier evaluates actual coverage and evidence sufficiency, may create isolated probes, and does not become the implementer.
4. **Disposition:** accept proven obligations; return demonstrated defects for authorized correction; request justified missing evidence; keep environment failures and unresolved contradictions distinguishable; retain non-blocking advisories.
5. **Rework economically:** retain source and durable records; produce a new identified package when needed; evaluate changed impact and carry forward previously valid evidence by an explicit admissibility decision, not by relabeling old PASS.
6. **Handle blocks and disputes:** perform bounded in-scope diagnosis, hold only materially affected claims, continue unrelated work as authorized; escalate scope blockers and use ADC-independent adjudication for genuine consequential disputes, notifying operator when initiated and resolved.
7. **Complete truthfully:** accept only obligations satisfied for the current package; provide operator-facing evidence and unresolved advisories. Technical completion does not automatically record operator acceptance or publication rights.

Examples of efficient paths: a true comment-only change may need only impact inspection; behaviorally inert mechanical code could require a build and assessment; a localized defect may require targeted regression; a shared authorization or concurrency change may require broad negative/integration evidence. These examples do not override a mandatory policy-specified check. A build cannot prove runtime behavior that the governing criterion requires observed.

### 6.1 Questions for focused independent review

1. Does this candidate accidentally create a second assurance-policy owner or overlap ADC-07 adjudicator authority?
2. Is the distinction between *mandatory evidence*, *optional diagnostic work*, *sufficiency judgement*, and *governing-check changes* unambiguous?
