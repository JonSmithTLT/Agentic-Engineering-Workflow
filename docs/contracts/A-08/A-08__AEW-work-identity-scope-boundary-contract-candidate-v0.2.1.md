# AEW Work Identity and Scope Boundary Contract — Candidate v0.2.1

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** PRINCIPAL DESIGNER CANDIDATE — NOT ADOPTED; NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION DESIGN  
**Authority:** User/operator remains the sole architectural adoption authority.  
**Intent:** Stable, implementation-independent work and subject identity for engineering **and** standalone software research, without a rival authority registry or universal lifecycle.

## 1. Place in the contract architecture

This subordinate boundary specializes the adopted Foundational Requirements v0.4, Product Cohesion/Operator Experience amendment v0.3, Research/Experimentation/Unattended Missions amendment v0.3.1, Engineering Workflow Umbrella v0.4, ADC v0.3.1, HEB v0.3.1, and AEV v0.3.1. **All seven remain authoritative; this proposal neither supersedes them nor modifies an existing operational control.**

A foundational amendment consolidation may be useful at the end of architectural discovery. It is tracked as document hygiene, **not** a prerequisite for defining this boundary and not an excuse to create a separate identity authority.

### 1.1 Normative ownership limits

- **Accepted authority, classification, scope-change eligibility:** ADC and the accepted objective. Work Identity records the referent and provenance; it does not authorize changes.
- **Execution identity, attachment, effects, revocation:** HEB. A persistent work ID cannot revive a revoked grant.
- **Evidence eligibility and assurance dispositions:** AEV. Work Identity makes exact subjects referenceable; it does not decide PASS or completion.
- **Progression, dependency decisions and integration actions:** forthcoming Coordination contract, under existing adopted obligations.
- **Knowledge admission, recall and claim relevance:** forthcoming Knowledge boundary, under existing provenance/authority rules.
- **API/site representation and operator actions:** forthcoming product-interface contracts; no new frontend authority here.

### 1.2 Conceptual vocabulary (not a schema)

| Concept | Governing meaning | Not equivalent to |
|---|---|---|
| Epic | Broad accepted initiative with outcome obligations | A sum of child statuses |
| Story | Assessable capability or bounded outcome | A folder of Tickets |
| Ticket | Bounded engineering assignment | A worktree or a build |
| Research inquiry | Accepted investigative question/purpose; may be standalone | An engineering Ticket |
| Experiment/episode | Attributable bounded investigation action/observation context | A new execution grant or production package |
| Hypothesis/claim | A qualified or unqualified proposition about a stated subject/conditions | An adopted requirement or independently authorized job |
| Accepted work revision | Immutable accepted scope/criteria applicable at a point | A mutable implementation snapshot |
| Implementation/integration package | Exact offered code/artifact/composed subject and material inputs | The work's objective or mutable branch name |
| Assurance determination | Attributable assessment of specified obligation/claim and governing basis for an exact subject, ordered within relevant applicability, with explicit qualified resolution links when supersession is asserted | The package itself, a globally superseding timestamp or a timeless boolean |

**An accepted revision is a new referent for scope/grant applicability and assurance-basis assessment, not a new permission or automatic reapproval demand.** The existing ADC/HEB/AEV authorities own those decisions; unchanged-scope applicability may be established cheaply and mechanically where qualified. Identity continuation versus successor classification is a lineage decision, not an effects loophole.

**Identity is not a mandate for one record, one hash, one service or one manually authored form per row.** A referenceable subclaim need not become a new top-level work item.

## 3. Required native producer–consumer interfaces (candidate obligations, not wire formats)

| Producer / governing source | Consumer | Required semantics | No-authority/uncertainty condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accepted objectives / competent authority | Admission, planning, Work Identity | Stable work ID, accepted scope/revision, parent commitments, effective criteria | Unknown/ambiguous accepted revision cannot be guessed or delegated to implementation |
| Work Identity and accepted work history | ADC, HEB, AEV admission | Accepted revision and cumulative scope referents for classified effects/grant applicability and derived mandatory assurance basis | A new accepted revision prompts applicability assessment; same ID never restores rights or waives newly governing checks |
| Package producer + protected or independently qualified observer | AEV verifier | Exact observed package closure and relevant inputs/outputs, assessed work revision and limits of observation | Producer-authored omission of a material dependency cannot establish an exact package or qualify PASS |
| AEV | Coordination and integration | Attributable, scoped determinations with subject, assessed obligation/claim, basis, applicability, and explicit qualified resolution links to named earlier determinations where supersession is asserted; otherwise preserve coexistence and unresolved contradictions | Neither a stale PASS nor an unlinked newer PASS can clear an applicable HOLD; unrelated determinations coexist; authorized applicability changes remain distinct |
| Research inquiries, consequential premises, claims and experiments | Knowledge, Coordination and engineering admission | Proposition/conditions, observations, qualification, receiving accepted purpose and recorded consequential consumer/premise links permitting reverse impact analysis | New research claims neither silently grant production authority nor erase earlier consumer reliance |
| Work/Research/Assurance/Coordination | API, website and supported operator clients | Selected canonical IDs, applicable parallel assurance scopes, currentness per claim, historical relationships, affected premises, relevant holds and permitted actions | API/site has no independent authority; ambiguous or stale scope cannot be rendered as a global PASS |

These are architectural obligations, not a requirement to expose every internal field. Consumer-specific authorization, freshness ordering, projection, error behavior and available actions are to be developed under Coordination and API contracts, per `PC-FR-06` and `OI-FR-07`.

## 5. Open questions for independent review and operator elicitation

1. **Identity continuation and cumulative drift.** Can an independent implementer check cumulative revisions against the originating accepted purpose while permitting the competent authority to decide continuation versus successor, without treating either label as a new grant?
2. **Parent revision and accepted child commitment.** Can independently implemented children retain historic acceptance/evidence while newly added parent requirements are demonstrably unmet and require new authorized work?
3. **Research granularity.** Can distinct claims be revisited and contradicted without one Ticket per hypothesis, a global claims service or a universal life cycle? Are content/condition changes sufficiently distinguishable?
4. **Qualified package closure.** Is material input/output identity sufficiently observable and independently qualified without mandating impractical whole-repository or perfect syscall-level tracing? Are unresolved limits truthfully recorded?
5. **Single authority source.** Are all guarantees clear about identity references versus authority/classification/assurance/progression/knowledge, so this contract cannot become another policy engine?
6. **Parallel assurance and consumer impact.** Can consumers retain a security HOLD alongside a later unrelated PASS, and find the consequences of a revised research premise without inventing their own assurance authority?
7. **Recovery economy.** Can unchanged-effect and unchanged-basis assessments be automated for a five-minute Ticket revision while preserving the mandatory gate for genuine scope expansion?
