# AEW Coordination and Native Integration Boundary Contract — Candidate v0.2.2

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** PRINCIPAL DESIGNER CANDIDATE — NOT ADOPTED; NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION DESIGN  
**Authority:** The operator alone adopts architecture. Existing operational controls remain.

## 1. Purpose and adopted hierarchy

This proposed domain boundary defines how AEW coordinates authorized engineering and research work, qualified discoveries, dependencies, and composed outcomes **without becoming a second authority, evidence, identity, or execution system**. It derives from eight adopted normative sources: Foundational Requirements v0.4 and two additive foundational amendments, Engineering Workflow Umbrella v0.4, ADC v0.3.1, HEB v0.3.1, AEV v0.3.1, and WIS v0.2.1.

The old failure was not merely that individually successful features needed more integration tests; it was that necessary producer–consumer relationships were not designed natively from accepted requirements. The obligations here establish those relationships before affected implementation and require qualified end-to-end outcomes.

### 1.1 Normative ownership remains with existing contracts

- **ADC:** Scope, classification, operator adoption and decision eligibility; coordination may route requests but cannot create approval.
- **HEB:** Agent attachment, protected effects, authorization lifecycle, revocation and ongoing containment; a coordination message grants no new effects.
- **AEV:** Evidence qualification and assurance determinations; coordination consumes qualified decisions but cannot produce substitute PASS conclusions.
- **WIS:** Work/revision/package/claim/determination identity, premise-consumer lineage, and explicit qualified resolution links; coordination governs progression over those referents, not their meaning.
- **Product Cohesion/OI:** Accepted requirements and operator-authorized release scope determine the composed outcomes and supported operator journeys; the later API/site contracts specify consumer interface details.
- **Research amendment:** Inquiries may be standalone, with experimental effects under ADC/HEB and claim/evidence qualification under AEV.

### 1.2 What this does not prescribe

No central blackboard, event bus, database, issue tracker, polling cadence, webhook, scheduler algorithm, standup, special agent role, or extra approval apparatus is mandated. Existing historical cross-feature and live-coordination designs are evaluated as possible implementations, not normative anchors.

**Read contract-first:** This table records native required handoffs. It does not itself grant permission to change state or require exposing internal secrets via the API. The supported API/operator-interface contract will specify the exact consumer projections, authorization, failure and freshness semantics.


### 2.1 Composition and admission semantics

- **Composition eligibility** comes from accepted objectives, requirements, and authorized release scope. A late-discovered necessary relationship is reconciled through existing change control before its affected responsibilities are implemented; neither the implementer nor Coordinator can declare it optional to erase a consumer.
- **Dependency truth** is distinct from convenient routing state: class from accepted scope/composition; satisfaction from accepted content-identified interfaces and qualified evidence; conflict from current authorized effect conditions. A coordinator may cache or project these facts but cannot become their owner.
- **Action-time validity** applies when effects or material progression are actually admitted, not just when a planning decision was proposed. The implementation may use transactions, fencing, reservations, version conditions or other equivalent mechanisms; this boundary specifies only the observable no-conflicting-admission guarantee.
- **Intermediate engineering** is permitted under accepted dependency classes and qualified independent effect authority, but incomplete integration exists only as a **provisional candidate**, never an accepted source of truth. It cannot be placed at a ref/location that downstream work resolves as accepted input until the appropriate acceptance/admission and mandatory-basis gates are satisfied. Calling a shared accepted branch “preparatory” does not change its effect. The accepted-input designation and resolver are themselves protected shared state, not agent-editable routing preferences.
- **Holds and operator visibility** consume the appropriate owners' facts. AEV resolves assurance findings; ADC/HEB and other accepted owners resolve their own protected/dependency/resource holds. A website outage or absent event is not evidence that a held condition has cleared.

## 5. Unresolved questions for operator and independent architectural reviewers

1. **Admitted-action validity:** Are competing dispatch and integration admission races excluded without forcing a scheduler or transaction architecture? Are genuinely unsatisfiable waits distinguished from legitimate parallel development cycles?
2. **Mandatory-basis consumption:** Can no-HOLD-yet be distinguished from all-mandatory-PASS, while preserving preparatory engineering and obligation-specific qualified resolution?
3. **Research-to-engineering handoff:** Are accepted consuming premises recorded at actual engineering reliance, not merely on the original research offer, without automatic scope change or duplicate registries?
4. **Composition fidelity:** Can an implementing lineage falsely declare necessary integration not applicable, or change the accepted interface content beneath its label?
5. **Message/data boundary:** Are peer/research messages treated as untrusted information despite permitted workspace writes, while direct authorized technical communication stays cheap?
6. **Scope of Coordination:** Which progress/resources/recovery obligations should remain here, and which deserve an adjacent focused boundary to avoid duplicate authority?
7. **Interface allocation:** Are individual fact freshness, per-action ADC eligibility, unresolved remote effects and supported canonical/outage reads guaranteed to API/site consumers?
