# AEW P-01 — Project Understanding, Derived Maps and Context Boundary Contract

**Version:** v0.2.1 candidate · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Status:** PROPOSED NORMATIVE; NOT operator-adopted, not an activation/profile decision or implementation conformance report.

**Authoritative anchors:** AEW-FR-01, 03, 07–09, 14, 17–19, 22–23, 31, 33–34, 36–38; PC-FR-01–06; OI-FR-03/04/07; research amendment AEW-FR-41.i/44.b/d/f; adopted WIS, CNI, ADC, HEB, AEV, AOI, K-01 v0.3.3 and D-01 v0.3.2. Exact originals and source hashes appear in the pack. Historical T5/project-maps v0.5 is significant adopted-direction/as-built *evidence*; this proposal does not silently re-adopt its implementation mechanics.

## 1. Outcome and conceptual partition

An authorized agent should be able to locate its **current accepted objective**, governing requirements, relevant boundaries, material blockers, source/code/test references and qualified past observations quickly. AEW should provide inexpensive navigation and selectively deeper evidence without treating a generated index, graph, model narrative or search result as source truth or authority. Different authorized actors may be working at different source bases concurrently.

Four **logical capabilities**, not necessarily four services, cooperate:

1. **Canonical source/reference resolution.** The owner of each project requirement, work revision, dependency/assurance disposition, repository/source artifact or qualified research observation remains its existing domain owner. P-01 is not a new authorizer.
2. **Derived project navigation.** Deterministic structure, optional language/build semantic extraction, test/constraint locators and attributed investigator synthesis have distinguishable claim classes and support.
3. **Bounded project-understanding query/context.** An authorized consumer requests and receives qualified, recipient-scoped, exact-basis material; the query layer is a relay/deriver and does not manufacture evidence.
4. **Owner-governed impact/consumption.** WIS/CNI/AEV/ADC decide accepted premise reliance, affected work, mandatory checks and effects. Knowledge K-01 handles admitted experiential advisory learning; maps are not Cases or Lessons merely because stored.

This contract specifies the target semantics independent of generator names, storage location, DB, graph implementation, MCP selection or API route. The historical T5 v0.5 detailed design may be preserved where it meets them. Exact wire and machine-readable provider/consumer fixtures are subordinate selected-profile work; no public website API is newly activated here.

## 3. Proposed atomic guarantees

### 3.1 Canonical orientation and source boundary

**P01-01 — Orientation minimum.** On an authorized initial assignment, resume or scoped handover, an authorized role MUST be able to obtain an economical orientation naming the effective accepted objective/revision, governing source locators, applicable work/authority limits, material dependency restrictions and the sources' qualification/currentness. The display MAY be brief with explicit bounded expansion; no full history or repository scan is mandatory.

**P01-02 — No secondary authority.** Maps, search indexes, summaries, architectural narratives, embeddings, candidate relationships and relevance/rank scores MUST remain nonauthoritative attributed derivations. They MUST NOT create/modify accepted requirements, WIS scope, ADC grants, AEV obligations/PASS/HOLD, CNI progression or Knowledge admission, including by being selected as a preferred map.

**P01-03 — Source resolution.** A returned authoritative reference MUST resolve, for the effective authorized recipient, to its competent source owner, exact subject/revision or qualified historical basis and observation/applicability condition. Failure to resolve MUST be represented without substituting a model summary as governing fact. An external non-repo authority MAY remain an external source; no forced repository copy becomes normative.

**P01-04 — Per-consumer identity and snapshot.** Each consequential query/orientation MUST identify the accepted assignment/work revision, source basis (committed revision/tree, independently qualified content-addressed working-copy overlay snapshot, or other source class), effective recipient/authorization context, and applicable build/configuration context when relevant. An overlay's identity MUST bind the exact observed bytes and relevant path membership at observation time; it MUST NOT be represented as a stable/current basis after mutation without reobservation. A globally selected map MUST NOT silently masquerade as current for a different Ticket's checkout, worktree or overlay.

**P01-05 — Qualified work restrictions.** Material accepted dependencies, interface commitments, unresolved HOLDs and other current owner-qualified work restrictions MUST be exposed in an assignment/decision context via WIS/CNI/AEV or a source-qualified reference **in the recipient-safe form required by AOI/AEV**. A P-01 cache cannot determine their absence merely from silence or a stale snapshot. Presenting protected details to an unauthorized consumer is forbidden; inability to disclose does not mean no restriction exists.

**P01-06 — Scoped disclosure and absence.** A role receives only records and derived conclusions authorized for its effective principal, assignment, root lineage and subject; provenance, counts, ordering, denial form, index hits and timing-sensitive metadata MUST NOT leak protected restricted inputs. A map/locator service inherits no operator or verifier privilege from its service credential. A qualified negative/empty response MUST be scoped to both the covered source/configuration domain **and the effective-recipient disclosure domain**; inaccessible members cannot be counted, named, or inferred through result status or claimed global closure.

**P01-07 — History/currentness separation and competent renewal.** Historical exact-basis maps and contextual references MAY remain usable for labelled navigation while current validity is stale or unknown. Historical source authenticity, present currentness, present applicability and recipient completeness MUST be distinct claims; a historical observation is not a current effect-time clearance. **Only the competent governing source owner** can qualify present-current renewal, finite continuity or set-closure for its mutable governing facts. A derived view can attest its own intact content and observed input versions, and may shorten supported validity, but its locally invented timer or notice silence MUST NOT renew, extend or replace the governing owner's witness. Immutable exact-object comparisons may support precisely bounded structural claims without asserting current mutable WIS/CNI/AEV authority.

### 3.2 Derived structural mapping

**P01-08 — Deterministic structural claim ceiling.** A structural map MAY assert bounded repository shape, tracked paths, language/build descriptors and declared/conventional candidates derived from exact observed source inputs. It MUST NOT assert actual runtime behavior, semantic call relationships, project intent, owner approval or protection of generated/vendor paths from its heuristics.

**P01-09 — Reproducible source/input set and authenticated production.** A structural artifact MUST identify exact source revision/tree/object kind, generator/ruleset version, actual material input identities, known omitted/unsupported portions and artifact identity. An implementation claiming deterministic input-sensitive freshness MUST account for every material file/blob it actually reads; mutation of an unrecorded supposed non-input must not change the claimed deterministic output. Self-described manifest fields, digest or producer name are **claims**, not proof of the actual writer/producer or of an authorized evidence class; use P01-37.

**P01-10 — Safe input handling.** Generation MUST treat repository objects, metadata, Git configuration, symlinks, LFS/submodule references, hooks, build descriptors and tools as untrusted where agent-controlled. Passive inspection MUST NOT silently execute repository-controlled code or gain protected privileges; any effective execution requires the relevant ADC/HEB experimental/tool authority. A repository-controlled filter cannot change what is represented as protected observed source bytes.

**P01-11 — Structural freshness precision.** The structural map's present-applicability result MUST compare qualified material inputs and tracked-path membership against the requested source basis; missing objects, noncomparable revisions and unverifiable sources yield UNKNOWN/PARTIAL rather than current. Ordinary code-content edits that cannot affect a declared structural output need not invalidate that output **only when nondependence is independently and mechanically derived from the complete recorded input and generator dependency basis, not asserted by the artifact writer**.

**P01-12 — Bounded outputs.** Structural generation, selection and queries MUST declare meaningful bounds/omission counts and permit efficient smaller-scope navigation; a cap-induced omission is not evidence of absent project elements. Unsupported or exceptionally large repositories remain honest about supported operational profile and observed performance.

### 3.3 Optional semantic observation and exact relation queries

**P01-13 — Distinct capability.** Semantic extensions MUST declare supported languages, relations, confidence/observation classes, extraction quality/coverage and unsupported relations. A language-neutral structural map need not construct semantic graphs. `NOT_SUPPORTED` differs from complete covered empty results.

**P01-14 — Configuration-qualified subject.** A semantic fact MUST bind to an exact relevant source, build/toolchain and resolution configuration, including per-translation-unit or equivalent context where distinctions matter. A query spanning incompatible configurations MUST present separated results or an explicitly labelled qualified derived union/intersection, never an unlabeled merged program.

**P01-15 — Dependency completeness and invalidation.** Where a semantic claim relies on dependency/search resolution, freshness MUST account for material positive AND negative dependencies (e.g., newly added earlier search-root header), generated inputs, compile database provenance and toolchain/extractor identity as applicable. Partial/noncomparable source or missing material closure downgrades the corresponding relation instead of reporting CURRENT.

**P01-16 — Semantics are observed-within-bounds.** `compiler_known` or equivalent means **qualified observation under the stated actual recorded compile context and source/writer provenance**, not proven deployed binary behavior; static inferences and investigator interpretations MUST be distinguishable. An agent-written edge claim, a generator-ID string, a declared CURRENT label or an unauthenticated file path cannot upgrade a graph's evidence class. Semantic outputs in unqualified agent-reachable storage cannot claim compiler-known/closure merely by internal metadata; use P01-37.

**P01-17 — Typed bounded queries.** Selected semantic capability profiles MUST provide unambiguous subject resolution and bounded relation/evidence expansion with observed source locators, touched-unit basis, configuration, support/coverage, freshness, terminal outcome and omission reason. Candidate search ranking only proposes locators and MUST NOT by itself certify relationships or causal explanations.

**P01-18 — Honest negative results.** An empty, `NOT_FOUND`, zero-caller, missing-dependency or radius-limited answer MUST identify the exact **profile-qualified source/configuration/relation/completeness domain and recipient disclosure domain** in which absence is supported. Insufficient source or recipient-visible closure yields `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `WITHHELD` or `EXHAUSTED_BUDGET` as applicable, rather than global absence; withheld details MUST NOT leak via the chosen status. A negative impact determination cannot be based solely on an incomplete map.

**P01-19 — Extractor isolation and build-metadata hardening.** Any semantic analyzer, compiler, parser, build configuration generator or helper processing potentially hostile project inputs MUST execute in the applicable qualified HEB/ADC inspection or effect profile. Repository-supplied `compile_commands.json` and equivalent build metadata MUST be parsed as untrusted data, never shell-executed, and reduced to a qualified allowlisted argv/working-directory/flag profile; untrusted compiler-launcher wrappers, repository `@response` expansion and execution/plugin-loading options (including `-fplugin`, `-Xclang -load`, `-B` and `-specs` or equivalents) MUST be denied unless independently qualified under an expressly selected effect profile. Approved compile argv MUST also confine file-reading arguments (including `-I`, `-isystem`, `-include`, `-imacros`, `--sysroot` and equivalent indirect/configured include routes) to qualified, recipient-permitted roots after path resolution; symlinks, defaults, implicit search paths and wrappers MUST NOT escape that boundary into private host files. Unsafe or unsupported units yield an attributable `NOT_SUPPORTED`/refusal, **not** an empty semantic fact, while independently safe units MAY proceed. The qualified extraction authority is limited to observations actually supported by its controlled inputs; analyzer identity alone does not qualify output or bypass verifier independence.

### 3.4 Synthesis, derived artifacts, selection and change

**P01-20 — Attributed architecture synthesis.** An investigator-produced architecture map MUST distinguish exact source-derived observations, qualified owner facts and attributed interpretation/uncertainty, with claim-specific locators and source basis. Selection or Lead review alone does not certify code semantics or accept new engineering requirements.

**P01-21 — Separate derived-state revision and protected selection.** Selection/current-navigation pointers and rebuildable indexes MUST be distinguishable from canonical work/authority revisions and carry stable source/artifact identities and attributable changes. A map refresh MUST NOT implicitly advance accepted work or invalidate unrelated grants. Shared publishing, selecting or replacing an automatically preferred derived view is a **separate integrity/authorization-sensitive derived-state effect** under P01-38; mere attributable files or pointers do not qualify default context. This DOES NOT require the historical T5 map-registry layout or `map_service` principal.

**P01-22 — Qualified composition.** Combining structural, semantic, constraint, test and architecture views MUST disclose exact source/configuration compatibility and separately qualify each claim's contributing versions. Components that cannot be shown comparable MAY be shown separately as historical/partial; they MUST NOT be combined into an apparently current uniform source.

**P01-23 — Recoverable derived records.** Immutable/pinned map artifacts needed to reconstruct an actual relied-upon context or conformance evidence MUST retain integrity and basis for the required lifecycle; otherwise derived caches MAY be regenerated or garbage-collected without deleting canonical evidence, work history, authority or K-01 records. Regeneration must never counterfeit a lost original observation/selection. Minimal attributable **delivered-only exposure provenance** required by P01-30/42 (exact packet identity, recoverable source/claim version membership and qualified delivery/recipient-cohort basis) MUST remain recoverable for the applicable active/unmerged attention lifecycle independently of whether optional full-map caches are retained; authorization, disclosure and retention limits remain with competent owners. Loss/unqualification of such evidence yields `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, not no exposure.

**P01-24 — Owner-qualified changed-input reconciliation.** The competent source owner alone qualifies currentness/continuity/closure of mutable governing inputs; map owners MAY maintain source-bound comparison, invalidate or narrow their own derived claims, and offer a limited historical navigation window, but MUST NOT declare mutable governing facts CURRENT on their own timeout. Relevant consumers MUST reconcile changed source/accepted work/owner fact versions before consequential reliance even when push events are late, lost, duplicated or out of order; a qualified owner witness, comparable snapshot or at-use owner check supports each positive current claim according to that claim's required assurance. Unknown comparability/owner silence cannot be promoted to success. Known delivered context before accepted premise reliance is addressed independently by P01-42. Not every change must invalidate unrelated sections or block unaffected work.

### 3.5 Context routing, role relevance and downstream use

**P01-25 — Cheap targeted retrieval first.** An authorized query path SHOULD preferentially use exact/cheap source locators and **independently qualified** deterministic map facts for suitable requests, with deliberate bounded expansion or independent investigation when they fail. Agent-reachable-derived artifacts without provenance/recomputation qualification MAY be presented as attributed tentative navigation, not as qualified facts or review scope. Model reasoning is permitted for explanation and judgment but cannot silently turn retrieval/synthesis into qualified evidence.

**P01-26 — Context budget, provenance and typed restrictions.** Role context MUST prioritize accepted objective/authority/safety obligations and relevant canonical state over optional maps, before optional Knowledge and other derived expansions as permitted. Every consequential context item MUST identify its source basis, provenance and known stale/partial state. **Mandatory accepted objective and applicable restriction references MUST be constructed as typed owner-derived items**, with effective-recipient source qualification and membership/completeness proof under P01-39, not synthesized into apparent completeness by an interested context assembler. Truncation/omitted applicability restrictions MUST be explicit or the purported actionable item withheld; recipient-restricted details MUST NOT be disclosed to achieve apparent completeness.

**P01-27 — Role-local relevance, independence and continuation.** An authorized Lead, worker, verifier or fresh replacement MUST be able to obtain task-targeted context without exhaustive transcript replay. Effective scope, review independence and disclosure remain role-specific; workers' context need not contain verifier-private drafts, and context inheritance never extends execution grant authority. Derived artifacts contributed by the work/lineage under review MUST be marked interested and cannot replace independent AEV/D-02 governing review scope or a direct source/delta path (P01-40).

**P01-28 — Knowledge separation.** P-01 MAY link or rank discoverable K-01 admitted Cases/Lessons and original guarded history according to qualified K-01 policy; it MUST carry Knowledge's actual source/qualification/disposition and cannot admit, waive, upgrade or alter Knowledge by map selection or use. Duplicate storage/index convenience does not create a second Knowledge authority.

**P01-29 — No ambient unbounded graph.** Query/context consumers MUST NOT require the entire repository map, graph or stored history in every agent prompt. An explicit qualified expansion path is required for wider investigation, with bounded cost, affected sources and failure conditions made visible. Evaluation may inform default budgets but not erase disclosure or source qualification.

**P01-30 — Context-delivery identity and exposure boundaries.** Where launch/rehydration depends on a context packet, its exact assembled version, substantive source bindings and qualified preparation/delivery evidence MUST be attributable without equating packet creation, attempted send, protected ingress ACK, actual model attention and accepted WIS premise reliance. The applicable route MUST declare how delivery/recipient coverage is qualified or explicitly unknown under P01-42. A stale/corrupt map or optional index alone MUST NOT revoke valid work/launch, while any lost mandatory accepted-objective/authority premise is governed by its actual owner and existing safety rules. A regenerated *different* packet must not be mislabeled one actually supplied earlier.

**P01-31 — Advisory impact only.** P-01 MAY provide candidate affected files, symbols, tests, interfaces, constraints or dependence paths with confidence/coverage, but WIS/CNI/AEV govern actual impact/accepted-premise, required checks and progression. Lack of a map edge MUST NOT lower risk class, waive a mandatory test, clear a HOLD or certify unaffected work; positive discoveries MAY trigger owner assessment.

**P01-32 — No autonomous scope or instruction effects.** Map creation, selection, retrieval, architecture summarization, impact suggestions and tool recommendations MUST NOT themselves authorize code edits, policy changes, grants, workflow promotion or experiments. Any effect uses applicable ADC/HEB controls and scope. Repository/agent-authored metadata, names and narrative remain inert untrusted data across prompts, CLI, sites and command construction as specified by P01-41; they cannot acquire authority or redirect execution merely by being included in or selected for context.

**P01-33 — Operator/other consumer truthfulness.** Where an operator or independently implemented site is committed to project-understanding visibility, the source-qualified map status, **observed writer and verification class**, exact provenance, version, known gaps, recipient-scoped negative-closure basis and authorized resolvers MUST be available through its applicable contracted interface; website cannot invent 'current', verified, safe or ready semantics. An agent-authored map cannot become a qualified website projection by self-labeling. D-01 remains the adopted initial GET scope; new P-01 website projections require their own consumer/release decisions.

### 3.6 Activation, suitability and evidence

**P01-34 — Profiles and graceful degradation.** A selected profile MUST identify which capabilities (authoritative retrieval, structure, language semantics, architecture synthesis, test/constraint locators, context packet, impact) are actually supported and at which source/deployment limits. Absent maps degrade to direct owner-qualified source retrieval and usable work, not to false success or gratuitous refusal; an inability to recover mandatory authoritative state remains subject to its existing owner boundary.

**P01-35 — Implementer substitutability.** Before a selected capability is claimed independently implementable/released, the applicable profile MUST define exact typed subjects, source/epoch/identity and recipient binding, operations, errors, concurrency/freshness semantics, bounded pagination/truncation, disclosure and provider/consumer fixtures. A historical T5 command name or old code behavior cannot substitute for the shared interface.

**P01-36 — Measured inclusion and qualification.** Evaluate claims of efficient/valuable project understanding against a declared smaller baseline (e.g., precise source search and grep), including resolution accuracy, missed material dependencies, context tokens, latency, cost, task quality and retrieval contamination. Authoritative-source handling, nondisclosure and nonauthority are mandatory even where a heuristic appears useful. Design scenarios and schema-valid examples are NOT executed qualification.

### 3.7 Derived integrity, assembled-context assurance and delivered-only exposure

**P01-37 — Evidence class from observed production, never self-label.** A derived artifact's effective provenance/evidence class MUST follow the **qualified observed writer/producer path, independent check and exact input basis**, not metadata the artifact can supply about itself. Until a protected producer/writer and ingress are qualified, artifacts stored or selectable in agent-reachable mutable space are `AGENT_REACHABLE_DERIVED`, even if they carry plausible hashes, signed-looking headers, compiler IDs, closure flags or CURRENT labels; they MAY serve as attributed exploratory leads only. An independently executed, appropriately integrity-qualified deterministic recomputation over authentic exact inputs using a qualified generator/ruleset MAY qualify **only the matched structural claims and exact snapshot** without protected persistent map custody. That recomputation must be independent of the artifact's mutable verdict, able to detect mismatch/substitution, and MUST NOT grant semantic, owner-currentness, cross-config closure, reviewer independence or shared selection authority. No agent-written self-check is a qualification oracle. **Independent recomputation eligibility requires a verifier-controlled, independently attestable observation boundary outside the checked artifact author/root lineage’s write, substitution and runtime-control reach**, for the entire check and its delivered verdict. The protected basis MUST cover (a) the actual generator/checker and toolchain code, interpreters, libraries and dependencies; (b) effective execution configuration and inherited environment, including Git replacement/graft/alternate object mechanisms, executable search paths, loaders, interpreter extensions, hooks and filters; (c) a consistent, independently authenticated input-object identity and contents, checked against the competent source owner’s exact basis rather than an agent-supplied reference or manifest, including object byte/hash verification and detected overlay changes; and (d) isolation or equivalent evidence preventing the checked lineage from tampering with the running checker, observations or response. An agent-controlled command invocation, same-UID process, editable verifier, injected library, mutable Git redirection, forged recomputation receipt or nominally isolated container MUST NOT by itself establish independence. A profile MAY qualify a protected off-guest checker or a locally qualified runtime with immutable toolchain/configuration, verified input closure and independently established runtime noninterference; it MUST provide adverse substitution/forgery tests for the actual host. A configured `ptrace_scope` value, process absence or root-owned executable alone is **not** proof that all these predicates hold. If any required protection, input comparability or checker-result authenticity is missing or cannot be verified, retain `AGENT_REACHABLE_DERIVED` and allow attributed exploratory navigation, without a deterministic-class upgrade.

**P01-38 — Shared derived publication and selection admission.** Publishing, selecting, replacing or making a derived artifact eligible as **shared default context for other assignments/consumers** MUST have a scoped, authorized, integrity-qualified admission/commit boundary distinct from arbitrary file writes and agent recommendations. The competent issuer/commit path MUST bind the actual actor, effective grant/lineage/generation where applicable, project/capability/recipient-scope, exact content digest, source basis, admissible evidence class, selection predecessor/version and a protected compare-and-set/fencing equivalent; stale/revoked/replayed/conflicting authorization, modified content or incomplete qualification MUST refuse or hold the shared-default effect. No particular daemon/account/DB is prescribed; if no protected admission exists on the native VM, local untrusted maps and independent at-use recomputation remain available, but **shared qualified selection does not**. Selection itself never qualifies source truth or changes WIS/CNI/AEV/K-01 authority.

**P01-39 — Owner-derived context membership and delivery verification.** Mandatory accepted objective and applicable restriction references in consequential orientation/context MUST be **typed copies or exact owner-issued recipient-safe references**, with subject/revision/owner, basis and qualification, rather than only an assembler's summary. At qualified delivery **when completeness is claimed for that delivery**, and again at a later consequential acceptance or effect admission for all then-applicable mutable owner conditions, a check outside the interested assembler's unilateral control (e.g., qualified ingress/owner resolver or a consumer-direct owner comparison) MUST establish equality/completeness against the **current applicable owner-qualified recipient-visible set** for the exact accepted work revision, including comparable roster/version closure without leaking withheld details. If the owner cannot provide an entitled safe completeness witness, or the check fails, mark the packet `INCOMPLETE`/`NOT_ESTABLISHED` and re-resolve owner facts before consequence-bearing use; a fluent summary, zero visible items or omitted HOLD is not completeness. A protected hidden restriction may be reflected only via the AOI/AEV permitted form, not exposed verbatim. A historical delivery-time equality result cannot substitute for a present owner-witness/currentness check; the consumer-direct owner query is no more qualified than that owner’s actually protected record and disclosure boundary under applicable G1–G4 gates.

**P01-40 — Interested lineage in independent review.** For a reviewer/assessor, any map, architecture synthesis or contextual claim authored, selected or materially controlled by the lineage or work under review MUST be explicitly marked **interested/provisional**, not independently qualified merely by retrieval. Its presence MUST NOT narrow the assessor's governing subject, cumulative material delta, accepted checks, or independent discovery route: use applicable AEV/D-02 original records and source inspection for scope. An assessor MAY consider the attributed derived artifact as a lead while honoring applicable ADC/AEV independence and confidentiality requirements.

**P01-41 — Inert repository and derived text.** Repository/agent-controlled filenames, identifiers, comments, build metadata, snippets, map labels, architectural narratives and Knowledge references carried into prompts, CLI output, websites or tool arguments MUST be typed/delimited as untrusted **data**, not role/system instructions, policy, execution argv or admitted assurance. Consumers MUST use context-appropriate escaping/quoting and fixed command construction rather than evaluating/interpolating arbitrary strings; hostile role-like text, terminal controls, HTML/script or shell syntax cannot alter authority, review scope or instructions. A legitimate quoted source policy remains evidence to inspect, not a P-01-issued policy change.

**P01-42 — Protective attention for delivered-only context.** When a material source/disposition correction or withdrawal affects **known qualified delivered context** of active invocations/unmerged work **before WIS/CNI accepted-premise reliance**, the accountable context/ingress producer MUST make qualified exposure (exact packet/source revision, recipient/active scope, qualified delivery boundary and completeness status) available through an authorized bounded attention/reconciliation consumer, and the responsible Lead/acceptance owner MUST have a path to re-read, revalidate, restart, hold or continue according to current source/effect rules. Mere preparation, delivery attempt, model attention inference or absent APP feedback does not establish delivery or accepted reliance. Selected H/E/map/context routes MUST declare whether ACK/cohort closure is **actually qualified by the receiving ingress boundary** (not by the assembler or map producer); unknown or incomplete coverage MUST remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED` with bounded attention/revalidation behavior, never `no affected recipients`. Producer outages, duplicates, lost ACKs and later corrections require safe reconciliation. Do not fabricate WIS accepted premises, globally stop unrelated work, bypass effect-time ADC/HEB/CNI checks, or require a new notification bus. Where the same packet carries K-01 Knowledge, reuse the compatible protected exposure channel without conflating the owners.

## 4. Consistency, failure and reporting semantics

Present-current mutable governing conclusions require a **competent source-owner issued** comparable currentness/continuity/set-closure witness or owner-direct at-use check adequate to the claim. A P-01 timer, derived-state selection, self-authored producer manifest or absence of change notices cannot extend it. Exact immutable repository-object comparisons and qualified recomputation may separately justify named structural snapshot claims, not present mutable owner status. Mixed views expose claim/field owners, input epochs, recipient scope and downgrade reasons independently; original historical observations remain authentic even when present applicability becomes stale. Scoped nondependence requires mechanically verified generator-input dependency evidence.

A map records the exact inputs it analyzed, including content-addressed worktree overlays at observation time. Its *historical authenticity* may remain valid after changes, but its *present applicability* is STALE/UNKNOWN unless comparable qualified inputs/witnesses establish it. A mapped component cannot assert absent relations without qualified source/relation/build/recipient closure. A matching repository/map artifact ID does not prove actual writer integrity; independently recomputed structural claims qualify only as narrowly verified. Agent-reachable artifacts must be visibly identified as such for navigation and independent review.

Candidate query outcomes distinguish `SATISFIED`, `NOT_FOUND_WITH_CLOSURE`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `STALE`, `UNKNOWN_SOURCE`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `WITHHELD`, `EXHAUSTED_BUDGET`, `INCOMPARABLE_INPUTS`, `INCOMPLETE` and `NOT_ESTABLISHED` as **semantic categories**, not one final universal wire enum. `NOT_FOUND_WITH_CLOSURE` requires a named profile-qualified **configuration/relation/source completeness domain and an effective-recipient disclosure domain**, with an appropriate owner or bounded extractor witness. Apparent absence cannot arise from unsupported, unqualified, capped or inaccessible content; denial form must not leak forbidden existence.

A producer-observed change is not proof that a consumer reconciled it. For consequential work and assurance actions, the consumer must reconcile current WIS/CNI/AEV/ADC/HEB source and premise state, regardless of missed notification or packet ACK. Separately, P01-42 supports bounded attention for known exposed but not yet accepted context; absent ACK or roster closure is UNKNOWN exposure, **not** proof of unaffected work. Qualified packet preparation, qualified ingress delivery, inferred model attention, accepted governing reliance and verified outcome are distinct facts.
