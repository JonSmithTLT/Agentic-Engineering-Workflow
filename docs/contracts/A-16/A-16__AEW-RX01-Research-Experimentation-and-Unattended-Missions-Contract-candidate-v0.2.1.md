# AEW RX-01 — Research, Experiments, and Unattended Missions Boundary Contract v0.2.1

**Status:** PROPOSED NORMATIVE — **not** adopted, implemented, released or conformance-qualified. Focused v0.2.1 correction to cross-experiment claim comparability and competent challenge admission; adoption authority remains the operator.  
**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Authority:** Derived from 15 separately operator-adopted architectural sources, particularly the October 16 adopted Research/Experimentation/Unattended foundational amendment v0.3.1.  
**Revision basis:** exact v0.2 predecessor SHA-256 `d52f56c418371683a52fd3f15a95597233bfce3dced02e28bba5ec7cf3fc5513`; all 38 original obligation IDs retained. The two v0.2 independent reviews and earlier reviews are evidence, not adoption.
**Purpose:** Define independently implementable semantic boundaries and cross-feature obligations for genuine software research, authorized conditional experiments, unattended missions and reliable handoff of research results, without inventing a second work, grant, assurance, Knowledge or resource authority.

## 0. Authority, terminology and non-goals

The **research inquiry** has a stable canonical WIS work identity, an accepted question and delegated boundaries. A **research iteration** is an attributable exploration/update within the accepted purpose; it is not necessarily a new work item. A **hypothesis** is a provisional explanation and may be refuted, refined or unresolved. A **research claim** is an attributable statement with evidence, conditions and limits; its *qualification* is different from the inquiry's *disposition*. An **experiment** is an identified effect-bearing investigation, which may be disposable, project-local or shared/remote and needs current effect authorization. An **operator-reserved decision** is never satisfied by silence, report prose or an agent's personal confidence.

No RX-01 state is a second canonical Ticket, code-package, verification PASS, adopted requirement, grant, Knowledge admission, budget policy, attachment authority or protected-effect issuer. ADC owns effective delegation and material decisions; HEB protects effects and lifecycle; WIS owns work/accepted revision and reference semantics; AEV governs claim-quality evidence and independent assurance where applicable; CNI owns dependencies and coordinated consumption; K-01 governs Knowledge admission/recall; P-01 provides nonauthoritative source understanding; R-01 governs resource admission/accounting; AOI governs safe operator-facing projections and supported actions. All 15 adopted documents remain controlling if this candidate is refined.

No specific research daemon, hypothesis database, extra agent, mandated form, universal instrumentation ladder, model-based certification service or always-on background mission is required. Tool use must be proportional to the claim; low-cost source reading remains a valid positive path. Where the deployment is not qualified for a protected effect, RX-01 documents a **blocked** capability instead of implying the VM can enforce it. Historical two-pass research and as-built investigations are evidence only, not normative authority.

**Hard ceiling:** A research observation may be authentic but wrong as an inference; a valid experiment may fail its authorized trigger; a completed mission may leave outcomes unknown; a published report may be useful without being Knowledge-admitted or adopted. Consumers must not collapse these distinctions.

## 3. Required composed flows (candidate semantics, not concrete APIs)

**F1 — Standalone inquiry and useful negative outcome.** The operator authorizes a WIS research inquiry with no Ticket. A researcher retrieves bounded source evidence and tests two plausible explanations. One is refuted; the other remains incompletely supported because source coverage was capped. A qualified research disposition preserves evidence, closure limits, alternatives and the next unanswered check. A report goes to AOI, and an eligible K-01 nomination can be offered. No engineering PASS or implicit implementation is created.

**F2 — Conditional experiment back into research.** A researcher working within a current grant proposes a real-project diagnostic with fixed trigger, inputs, effect class, preregistered predictions/controls and accountable run cohort. HEB/ADC qualify pre-effect grant/effect scope; CNI separately admits current interference/dependency for shared/persistent mutable effects; R-01 applies where resource controls are selected. Trigger evidence is compared to the actual target and temporal policy at effect time. A safe scratch branch may proceed economically only under independently qualified disposable confinement. An independent pre-observation witness closes the commitment and run set before any result is claimed decisive; manual runs retain a separate untrusted-evidence path. Outcomes and potentially unqualified residue rejoin the inquiry; engineering promotion needs its own work/assurance authority.

**F3 — Unattended mission, reserved decision and lost message.** Before operator absence, an accepted mission identifies budget, effect envelope, report point and foreseeable decisions. An ambiguous consequential change is asked asynchronously through CNI/AOI; silence holds it. Independent source analysis proceeds. Reattachment/revocation does not reactivate stale work; failed delivery is reconciled from owner facts without a model watchdog. Monday report distinguishes executed evidence from proposals and residues.

**F4 — Contradiction propagates after consumption.** Engineering explicitly accepts research premise V (with source-qualified scope X), while a research author had also inferred unsupported scope Y; K-01 may separately have a conditional Lesson, and one active invocation has only a delivered context packet. Competent source correction or a source-owner/ingress-admissible material challenge narrows V; identical already-resolved unsupported allegations cannot repeatedly reset qualification without new material basis. Qualified ingress promptly marks affected scope challenged without waiting on beneficiary approval; final AEV/source resolution remains independently owned. WIS/CNI and K-01 reconcile accepted/exposed consumers by exact basis, no old response clears the newer challenge, and unknown ACK remains unknown. Effect-time holds apply only where relevant; safe unrelated work continues.

**F5 — Manual/host limitation.** An experiment cannot execute under an unqualified current VM profile, including same-UID scratch that cannot contain effects. It remains blocked instead of running through shell. If the operator separately executes, an independent ordering witness may preserve a contemporaneous commitment but actual run-set completeness and observation competence must still be qualified. HEB untrusted-contribution admission determines any subsequent protected use. Manual evidence and local inventory cannot acquire automatic provider, host, residue-clean or verification qualification.
