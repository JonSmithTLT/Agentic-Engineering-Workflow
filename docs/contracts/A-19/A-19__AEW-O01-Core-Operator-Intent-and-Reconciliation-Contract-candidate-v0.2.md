# AEW O-01 Core — Operator Request, Standing Policy and Reconciliation Boundary

**Version:** v0.2 CANDIDATE · **2026-10-17** · **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT ADOPTED. **Scope:** minimum logical command grammar supporting IF-13, resource-policy authorization and later operator-reserved lifecycle commands; not the completed universal O-01 command API.

## 1. Ownership and deliberate scope limit

O-01 is a **carrier and reconciliation contract**, not the human-origin issuer itself. Existing ADC/HEB govern consequential admission, AOI governs honest operator experience, R-01 governs spending classes; WIS/CNI/AEV retain their evidence and lifecycle boundaries. Qualified protected G3 origin and its concrete input channel **remain unavailable/unqualified on the selected shared-UID VM** unless separately demonstrated. Transport bearer tokens, startup unlock, a website click, claimed `actor=operator`, or a document in agent-writable files cannot assert G3. No POST/PATCH/DELETE is added to the adopted D-01 GET-only interface; protected transport is a separately selected future O-01 profile.

This slice covers typed intent/receipt/status for `PROFILE_CHANGE`, `STANDING_RESOURCE_POLICY` and `OPERATOR_LIFECYCLE` **as a command family designation only**, not a full lifecycle operation parameter contract. Actual close/drain/stop/custodian semantics stay with HEB/ADC. `PREFERENCE_DRAFT` is explicitly non-authoritative and may be locally edited without becoming a profile activation or policy decision.

## 2. Proposed obligations

**O01-01 — Distinct states and actors.** Intent authorship, authenticated human origin, operator decision, command admission, effect execution, effect observation, result-read success and client display SHALL be independent concepts. Carrier identity is not human-origin proof.

**O01-02 — Exact, resolvable action subject. Each request SHALL bind its source/subject/generation, client key and a **typed action-specific canonical target** or an immutable, competent-source-qualified dereferenceable target body, plus original predecessor/preconditions and versioned displayed basis. `PROFILE_CHANGE` SHALL bind target profile ID/definition revision and expected functional snapshot revision. `STANDING_RESOURCE_POLICY` SHALL bind exact policy decision ID, issuer/source record and content hash, parent/resource class, provider/account/recipient, bounds, effect/expiry interval, relevant current policy predecessor and operator acknowledgment of cooperative limitations; the *operator-issued* record remains external to the measured lineage. A naked `args_digest` or mutable unresolvable body SHALL NOT supply command meaning. Exact canonical target/body bytes (including source epoch and operation-specific preconditions) SHALL be bound identically into the displayed human meaning, original operation fingerprint, origin witness if required and admitted operation; changed or unverifiable bytes SHALL conflict/hold. O-01 Core SHALL NOT execute the `OPERATOR_LIFECYCLE` family absent later selected, typed operation bodies. (AOI-03.e, AOI-07.a–c; S01-14/22; R01-07; HEB-07.b.)

**O01-03 — Owner-derived direction and protected origin. The admitting owner SHALL derive each action's direction/authority effect from the typed governed operation and target, never from caller-supplied `cancel`, `stop` or `protection_increasing` labels. Authority-increasing/preserving actions (including releasing HOLD, resume, widening recipients or cap) require all applicable G3/ADC/HEB qualification; **operator-only authority-reducing** close, drain, stop-now, revoke, contain and narrow actions also require qualified human origin as explicitly required by adopted AOI-07.c and HEB-07.b. Protective actions use AOI's changed-basis anti-churn and bounded-scope rules *after* legitimate original consent, not a G3 bypass. A local operator may manually stop a process outside AEW and present the observation as untrusted external evidence, or invoke an expressly non-authoritative best-effort functional process-stop tool where selected; neither path grants AEW protected lifecycle disposition or asserts that remote effects/custody were stopped. Tool text/README cannot submit a typed command. (AOI-03.e/f, AOI-07.c/f/g; HEB-07.b/h; ADC-08.g.)

**O01-04 — Attestation and non-self-proof. An origin record SHALL bind the exact normalized typed target body or qualified immutable record, subject, predecessor/governing epochs and all relevant displayed consequences to its one-use intent. Qualified operator-only effects SHALL verify actual protected human display/input, correct issuer, current entitlement and outside-lineage single-use binding; `origin_verification=QUALIFIED_EXTERNAL` or `provenance.qualification=QUALIFIED_BY_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` in agent-writable JSON is only a *claim*, not proof. No locally issued functional stop or startup unlock may be projected as G3. (AOI-03.e/f, AOI-07.a/c; S01-15/31.)

**O01-05 — Current-source confirmation.** Protected authorization SHALL compare current operator role, explicit original decision, grant/generation, effective profile/policy revisions, resource class, recipient/source entitlement, and target basis at admission and effect time as applicable; missing/stale/contradictory proofs SHALL hold or deny, not silently accept.

**O01-06 — Canonical request and idempotency.** AEW's qualified request owner SHALL issue an immutable operation identity and bind a normalized original intent fingerprint; a client key alone cannot create a new origin or fresh cap/decision. Same-intent retry reconciles; same client key with changed arguments is conflict; unknown effect never retries blindly.

**O01-07 — Lifecycle vocabulary.** `RECEIVED`, `ORIGIN_PENDING`, `HELD`, `ADMITTED`, `EXECUTING`, `EFFECT_CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED`, `UNKNOWN_EFFECT`, `RECONCILING`, `SUPERSEDED` distinguish command admission from effect, and effect from result read. `EFFECT_CONFIRMED` needs source-qualified outcome, not a return-code or requested flag.

**O01-08 — Issued standing policy and cooperative path. A cooperative/non-hard paid-model class is an **explicit original operator governance decision external to the measured agent lineage**, identifying exact parent/resource class, provider/account/recipient, bounds, effective/expiry window, limits of enforcement and current comparable issuer/source basis (S01-14). The selected policy authority and read-only projection MAY support a genuinely operator-elected functional/cooperative mode, *if* the source-issued decision and applicable R-01/ADC/HEB/provider/disclosure gates can be competently compared at original model-call admission; neither the workplace's organizational account cap nor a projected local config alone qualifies the decision or smaller task caps. An independently verified provider/org account cap is a qualified external hard bound for **that exact account only**, not a universal prerequisite for *all* operator-elected cooperative classes unless selected operator policy requires it. On current host with no qualified G3, do not treat a worker-editable in-band website/CLI policy issuance or mutable config as a qualifying operator-only change. Permit external operator governance proposals/history and separately qualified read-only policy source binding; if current source comparability cannot be proved, AEW-controlled paid dispatch depending on the policy remains HELD. (S01-14, R01-09/43, AOI-03.e, ADC-08.)

**O01-09 — Policy comparison and revocation. An issued policy decision is immutable history; `revoked=false` on an old record SHALL NOT imply current entitlement. Current issuer-qualified replacement/revocation events and inherited mandatory parent caps govern effect-time comparability, independent of projection and timestamps. `MANDATORY_HARD` policy MUST declare a meaningful bounded numeric ceiling in its applicable scope, and no unknown/null bound or local SDK estimate can qualify hard-cap enforcement. A worker's local `cooperative=true`, an old apparently valid decision, changed provider or a narrower pending cap SHALL NOT override current mandatory constraints; unknown/conflicted sources hold affected paid effects. (S01-14; R01-07/09/43; AOI-03.d; ADC-08.f/i.)

**O01-10 — G3-unqualified command handling and practical stop. When all qualified operator-origin routes are unavailable, O-01 SHALL hold/refuse operator-only protected commands of **either direction**; it SHALL report `ORIGIN_PENDING`/`HELD` and owner/currentness limits rather than claiming protective authority. The operator SHALL retain a clear local manual process-stop path or a separately selected best-effort **unqualified functional** stop affordance that never alters canonical grants or falsely asserts close, drain, stopped remote effects, custodian clearance or verified source state. AEW may record that observation as external/untrusted until the governing source reconciles it; worker-forged command text cannot become even a typed stop request. A protected stop already admitted under a genuinely qualified issuer SHALL not be blocked merely by agent-induced basis churn in its authorized scope (AOI-07.c). (AOI-03.e/f, AOI-07.c/e/g, HEB-07.b/h, ADC-08.g.)

**O01-11 — Controlled transition ordering.** Before a profile change becomes effective, the competent controller SHALL compare the exact predecessor, in-flight admitted work, resource/recipient restrictions and profile-qualified capability evidence. A prior command receipt alone does not set effective state; IF-13 owns transition representation.

**O01-12 — Cancellation, loss, and held results.** Cancel requested is not cancellation achieved. After response loss, detach, timeout or restart, preserve original operation and any outstanding effect, spend, custodian and result; re-query by canonical operation ID and issuer fencing. Never reexecute based only on a lost receipt.

**O01-13 — Challenge and revocation.** A newer competent policy decision, entitlement revocation, source correction or protected negative observation SHALL fence stale approvals at relevant effect boundaries; no newest timestamp or self-authored status can restore an invalid grant.

**O01-14 — Shared reason vocabulary and projection. This slice SHALL consume and extend the **single governed AOI/D-01/reasons.py problem taxonomy** by separately versioned mapping, not mint a third independent refusal namespace. Candidate labels `G3_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `ORIGIN_INVALID`, `PRECONDITION_CHANGED`, `POLICY_NOT_COMPARABLE`, `CAPABILITY_NOT_QUALIFIED`, `ENTITLEMENT_DENIED`, `CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN_EFFECT`, and `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE` are provisional logical reasons, not wire-level competing `problem.type` identifiers. Recipient-safe projections SHALL preserve qualification source and non-disclosure. The selected typed error registry remains deferred and must include exact deterministic mapping fixtures. (AOI-03.c/d, S01-21, D-01.)

**O01-15 — One unlock remains non-authoritative. The single routine S01-31 startup unlock MAY prepare eligible normal services but SHALL NOT constitute G3, issue a standing paid policy, authorize close, widen recipient/cap, or otherwise grant protected operator authority. Explicit operator governance records are separately issued through their qualified applicable path, not from an unlocked agent-reachable runtime copy. A local manual/best-effort process stop requires truthful limited status and is not a qualified protected command. (S01-31; AOI-03.e; HEB-07.b.)

**O01-16 — Website independent implementation.** A site SHALL display user-requested, pending, admitted and effective states from sourced backend records rather than optimistic success. The site cannot mint origin evidence, and an operator click over shared UID cannot claim G3. GET-only D-01 remains the adopted read boundary until separate selected read/write contracts explicitly qualify protected routes.

**O01-17 — Source-specific request disclosure.** Receipts and projection SHALL preserve currentness/coverage/restriction and not leak hidden command existence, private policy scopes, secret material, or unrelated protected reviewer reservations via errors or progress updates.

**O01-18 — Selected transport, binding and owner precedence. A later selected O-01 profile SHALL freeze canonical target serialization and digest verification, typed action/version registry, bounded immutable source dereferencing and recipient disclosure, verified issuer/nonce binding, operation-specific predecessor compare, policy issuer/epoch/revocation and protected status mapping, retry/reconciliation/retention, actual guarded HTTP/IPC transport and independent provider/website/harness fixtures. The current record schema is a testable logical draft, not a selected G3 component or D-01 write extension. Incompatible or absent typed lifecycle bodies stay unsupported, not locally invented by an implementer. (AOI-07, HEB-07/08, S01-21, D-01.)

### Composition distinction: local functional process stop is not a protected lifecycle effect

An already-running same-UID process can be terminated through ordinary local OS/user tools even when AEW lacks G3. That is a **bounded functional/operator-external affordance**, not O-01 admitted close, protected authority revocation, or confirmed remote/async effect termination. AEW SHALL not hide access to ordinary operator controls or misdescribe their results; it also SHALL not issue a protected stop from agent-authored README/tool content. Where a selected unqualified local stop helper is offered, status is `BEST_EFFORT / ORIGIN_UNQUALIFIED / EFFECT_UNVERIFIED` until independently corroborated. A stop request whose effect clears a HOLD, withdraws custody, publishes results or modifies protected authority is **not** such a helper; apply AOI-07.c and the correct issuer.

## 3. Minimal handshake and truthful website journey

1. Operator sees entitled current effective profile/basis via D-01 or separately qualified read. A stale or incomplete view may display an editable *proposal* but no protected confirmation.
2. Operator selects target or requests a standing resource class; frontend authors only a `command_intent`, never an issuer claim.
3. For protected operator-only actions, a **separately qualified future O-01 origin component** verifies actual human presence, exact displayed consequence, current entitlement, replay binding and permitted current basis. If unqualified, return `ORIGIN_PENDING`/`HELD` and no protected action.
4. Competent owner returns `command_receipt` with source provenance; downstream IF-13 may enter `CHECKING`, `HELD`, `APPLYING` or `EFFECTIVE` only after conditional commitment. For resource policy, the qualified originating decision is first verified against its immutable governing source; R-01 validates current policy and scoped paid admission; for lifecycle, HEB/ADC own the specific operation.
5. Progress is reconciled by original canonical operation, generation and source version; updated UI labels follow authoritative receipts, not click timing. Profile switching does not migrate restricted content or secrets.

**No current trusted-command path exists merely because this handshake is specified.** A standing cooperative policy decision must be separately made through an operator-governance path and must have a competent, independently comparable current issuer/reference before AEW-controlled paid dispatch may rely on it; an uploaded Markdown note or writable TOML is not automatically an admitted runtime policy.

## 4. Failure and graceful continuation

| Failure | Safe outcome |
|---|---|
| Unlock succeeds; operator requests protected close using ordinary session cookie | `ORIGIN_PENDING/HELD` without G3; no protected close; local manual stop is distinct |
| Client retries same command after timeout | owner-bound original operation reconciled; no new grant/effect |
| Worker changes command args after origin attestation | digest mismatch → reject/hold; no repricing/rebinding |
| Old operator paid policy presented after narrower mandatory cap | compare current decision, deny paid class rather than honor old weaker selection |
| Gateway and G3 offline | held protected effects/commands; independently entitled safe source inspection remains |
| Website receives a receipt but effective state is still old | show admitted/pending separately; no false success |
| Revocation during transition | fence original authorization as adopted ADC/HEB require; reconcile already admitted effects separately |
