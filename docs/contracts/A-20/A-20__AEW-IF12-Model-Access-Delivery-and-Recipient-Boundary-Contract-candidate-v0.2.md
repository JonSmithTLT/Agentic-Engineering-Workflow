# AEW IF-12 — Model Access, Delivery and Recipient Boundary

**Candidate:** v0.2 · **2026-10-17** · **PROPOSED, NOT OPERATOR-ADOPTED**. **20 proposed obligations** (`IF12-01`–`IF12-20`). No model gateway, call, host, recipient or paid effect is approved or qualified by this document. Companion: IF-10 v0.2. Existing adopted ADC, HEB, AOI, S-01, IF-13, O-01, R-01, WIS and source-owner requirements prevail. The first-workplace integration constraint is the operator-selected OpenCode 2.0.18 + organization upstream-mTLS path, not a universal AEW architecture requirement.

## 1. Outcome, native owners, and exclusions

IF-12 is the **substitutable boundary for model request intent → authorized delivery → attributable provider response/unknown outcome**. It supports model/harness adapters for General Local and Work Rocky 8 without making the transport/gateway an authority for grants, policy, data ownership, resource accounts, assurance or operator consent.

| Information / decision | Canonical owner or qualified producer | Consumers and when |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible model/harness and effective endpoint/profile | Model-role/harness policy plus IF-13 effective profile source | Lead/router and IF-12, before dispatch and on version, SDK, adapter, account or route change |
| Work, assignment, grant and original paid operation | WIS/ADC/HEB competent admission owner; owner-derived equivalence, not client key | IF-12 and IF-10, at request, replay, cancel, detach and settlement |
| Source content, derivation and allowed recipient | Competent WIS/P-01/K-01/RX-01/AEV/project policy/source owners under S01-17/26 | Context assembler, delivery admission, logging and diagnostics, at every external disclosure |
| Resource policy, reservation and permitted cost class | Operator/O-01 policy plus R-01 qualified resource controller (IF-10) | IF-12 call admission, usage observation, operator read projection |
| Model transport, endpoint/account and provider response | Selected provider adapter; Work on-VM gateway is **transport/cooperative** unless independently qualified for more | Caller, reconciliation, IF-10; not an issuer of source/authority/grant |
| Charge and price/billing truth | Competent provider/account issuer or independently qualified meter for exact claim; IF-10 reconciles | R-01 budget scopes, WIS research/task attribution, website/CLI |
| Qualified effective human/operator commands | AOI/O-01 and separately qualified G3 | None inferred from session unlock, model request or gateway token |

**Deliberate non-links:** A model role profile is eligibility, not effect authority. An originating-mTLS gateway is not an AEW credential protection boundary on the shared-UID VM. Transport success is not paid admission, a provider response is not AEV proof, and external model content is untrusted until the responsible consumer qualifies it. IF-12 does not require a global event bus or one process topology.

## 2. Proposed normative obligations

**IF12-01 — Ownership and effect distinction.** The model-delivery profile SHALL implement original request/effect, exact recipient, source-disclosure and response semantics without creating its own operator policy, budget, grant, accepted-evidence or qualification authority. Model access is a potentially chargeable and externally disclosive effect even when it has no filesystem-write permission. *Basis: HEB-01/03; S01-04/17/24; ADC-08; R01-01/03.*

**IF12-02 — Original call identity.** Every AEW-controlled model call SHALL bind owner-qualified original operation intent and its equivalence, WIS work/revision and accountable parent scope, attachment/generation, assignment/lineage where applicable, model/harness/provider/account/recipient, selected IF-13 snapshot and exact requested action. User-generated request ID, idempotency key or lineage header is an alias, never a fresh authorized intent or spend-reset key. *Basis: R01-02/05/07/08; O01-06; HEB-07/08.*

**IF12-03 — Distinct identities and attempts.** Original call ID, dispatch attempt ID, provider request ID (if supplied), generation, response/stream ID and eventual billing item SHALL remain separate, with explicit mapping and source/coverage. Reconnect, retry or SDK substitution SHALL NOT recast the same uncertain call as a fresh authorized paid effect. *Basis: R01-08/32; HEB-08; IF13-13.*

**IF12-04 — Effective route qualification.** IF-12 SHALL resolve the actual effective route from the currently observed IF-13 profile, selected eligible model/harness, endpoint/account/credential and transport basis. A requested profile, compatible SDK, mutable adapter config or startup unlock does not qualify the effective protected route; route identity and relevant qualifiers are checked at dispatch, not only at setup. *Basis: S01-06/09/11/22/24; IF13-03/08/09/12.*

**IF12-05 — Workplace-originating mTLS.** The selected pinned OpenCode 2.0.18→organizational model API binding SHALL support a gateway/mediator that genuinely **originates upstream certificate-authenticated TLS** and interoperates with the supported OpenCode request, streaming and error protocol. It SHALL NOT be described as a generic CONNECT proxy or require all General Local installations to route through it. Other harness native-mTLS support remains independently selectable and unqualified until verified. *Basis: S01-06/11/28; operator-confirmed deployment constraint.*

**IF12-06 — Same-UID gateway honest claims.** For the operator-selected Work topology, the gateway and active key remain on the shared Rocky 8 VM, potentially reachable to the same-UID worker. It MAY function as transport/routing and cooperative usage observation; absent independent proof it SHALL report credential custody, non-bypassability, source-delivery enforcement and authoritative per-task metering as `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. At-rest passphrase protection and one startup unlock are hygiene and usability, not agent-resistant runtime protection. *Basis: S01-11/13/31; R01-08/43; HEB-03.*

**IF12-07 — General Local parity without company dependencies.** General Local SHALL support an eligible direct-provider route, offline/no-charge synthetic adapter or legitimately qualified local mediated route without organizational certificates, organization account or gateway. The applicable resource/disclosure/effect policy is identical in meaning, but its qualified implementation may differ. Functional success may be reported as such without manufacturing protected metering or effect status. *Basis: S01-05/27/28; IF13-19.*

**IF12-08 — Competent source-release decision or closed reachable-read set.** At every governed external model payload/fragment delivery, the actual provider/account/recipient, logging/retention destinations and applicable source/work-owner restrictions SHALL be qualified. Two alternative proof forms are eligible: **(a) source-derived form**, which establishes owner-qualified lineage, versions, restrictions and allowed recipients for every material original or derived fragment/tool result; or **(b) whole-readable-universe form**, which demonstrates **outside the measured agent lineage** that the *complete effective readable and introducible context* of that invocation (initial prompt, workspace, mounts, relevant process/runtime state and memory/history, accessible files, tools/MCP/search/retrieval results, environment, network and future dynamic additions) is bounded, unchanged through delivery or competently invalidated, and **every potentially disclosed member** is owner-qualified for the exact provider/account/recipient and retention/logging destinations. A container mount list, agent-authored index, worker-set `public` flag, profile string or authorized-to-read status alone cannot establish completeness or grant external disclosure. Either proof form SHALL be current at each actual disclosure and fail closed for the affected send when its coverage or comparable owner policy is missing, while unrelated permitted work continues. *Basis: S01-09/17/24/26; HEB-03.j/k; AOI-04; WIS/P01/K01/RX01.*

**IF12-09 — Mixed source and unknown universe composition.** The effective request, including summaries, paraphrase, embedded references, tool errors, agent memory and new context delivered in a continuing session, SHALL respect the intersection of restrictions over its qualified source-derived population **or** the complete independently bounded whole-readable-universe population admitted under IF12-08. Neither an agent-picked subset nor omission of a reachable privileged source makes a positive closed-universe claim. If any material readable or newly introduced content may have a more restrictive recipient entitlement, the whole-universe shortcut is invalid; use independently qualified per-fragment derivation or withhold the affected external delivery. On a same-UID unconfined Work process, do not infer closed coverage from workspace directory alone; no semantic DLP enforcement is claimed without actual eligible controls. Preserve independently permitted requests and safe source-only work. *Basis: S01-09/17/24/26; HEB-01.a/e; P01-42; K01-15.*

**IF12-10 — Delivery-time comparability.** Qualified data-release basis, effective provider/account, required resource admission, model selection and grant/generation SHALL be checked at **actual dispatch**; any reassembly, retry, fallback, changed credential, route, model, account or source epoch invokes the affected checks anew. A cached admission cannot authorize a different payload/recipient. *Basis: S01-22/24/26; IF13-09/14/17; R01-39.*

**IF12-11 — Conjunctive admission for all real external model delivery.** **Every** AEW-controlled request actually delivered to a remote/provider model—whether paid, prepaid, free-tier, zero-price, promotional-credit or unknown-chargeability—SHALL require current original-operation ADC/HEB effect permission, the source-owner-qualified release to the exact provider/account/recipient and logging destinations under IF12-08/09, the actually observed eligible route, applicable IF-10 resource policy (including request/rate/quota/concurrency limits and any possible monetary exposure), and relevant CNI effect-time interference admission. A zero price never makes an external send a no-effect fixture. Only a positively established **local deterministic no-network/no-external-effect** fixture MAY use the separate fixture path; worker-authored `chargeable=false`, `FUNCTIONAL_ONLY`, upstream account caps or successful TLS cannot waive another gate. Failure of one gate withholds only the affected effect; an IF-10 `MAY_EVALUATE_OTHER_GATES` result alone never authorizes dispatch. *Basis: HEB-01/03; ADC-08; S01-14/17/24/26; O01-08/09; R01-03/07/09/43.*

**IF12-12 — Dispatch/response versus qualified context ingress.** The delivery boundary SHALL distinguish `PREPARED`, `ADMISSION_HELD`, `DISPATCHING`, `SENT`, `STREAMING`, `COMPLETED_OBSERVED`, `FAILED_BEFORE_SEND`, `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, `RECONCILING`, and `SETTLED_OR_CLOSED` as model-provider effect states. **Separately**, when a K-01/P-01/other governed context packet is delivered into a model/agent invocation through a selected IF-12 path, the profile SHALL identify the **actual receiving-ingress-qualified producer** (IF-12 only if it genuinely hosts that protected ingress) and its packet/source-version membership, source epochs, original attempt, recipient/active-invocation/cohort, observer/coverage basis and stage `PREPARED`, `ATTEMPTED`, `DELIVERY_ACKNOWLEDGED` or `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. A provider `SENT`, streaming reply, assembler record or self-reported model attention is **not** that ingress ACK. The competent ingress SHALL make qualified delivered-only exposure and unknown/cohort coverage available to authorized K-01/P-01 source-withdrawal reconciliation and CNI/Lead attention **without creating WIS accepted reliance** or a global event bus. Lost, duplicate, stale or reordered packet/ACK and source revisions require owner-qualified reconciliation; unknown closure remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, never proof of nondelivery/no affected recipient. Local timeout is not proof of no remote effect or charge. *Basis: HEB-08; R01-08/32; K01-10/15/21/22; P01-23/30/42; O01-07/12.*

**IF12-13 — Lost response and non-idempotent retry.** No call with `SENT`/unknown remote execution may be automatically redispatched after timeout, detach, cancel or gateway recovery without competent provider evidence of no effect or a separately qualified idempotent mechanism bound to the same original intent. Reconcile by original operation/provider references; preserve outstanding uncertainty in IF-10. *Basis: HEB-08; R01-08/32; IF13-13.*

**IF12-14 — Cancellation truth.** `cancel_requested`, local stream disconnected, upstream cancellation observed and no-additional-charge proven are different claims. Stop/drain/revoke SHALL fence **new** work under ADC/HEB, preserve custodian/late results, and report actual provider effect/charge uncertainty; a local signal alone is not a protected operator close or settled cost. *Basis: HEB-07/08; R01-31/32/34; O01-10/12.*

**IF12-15 — Usage evidence, not settlement.** Gateway or harness token counts SHALL be typed as estimates/cooperative observations with source and method; they SHALL NOT release protected reservation, qualify per-task cost, or prove billing completeness unless IF-10 independently qualifies the actual non-bypassable meter for that exact provider/account/route and original call. Record absent, contradictory and out-of-order provider usage without overwriting earlier outstanding exposure. *Basis: R01-08/09/43; S01-13.*

**IF12-16 — Response provenance and untrusted content.** The response SHALL identify observable model/provider/route, original intent, attempt, stream and content scope where actually established. Provider text, function calls and code suggestions remain untrusted proposals, not operator instructions, accepted evidence, autonomous grant or source truth. Tool invocation from response requires separate applicable HEB/ADC/CNI admission. *Basis: HEB-01/03; ADC-08.g; AEV-03; RX01-11/12.*

**IF12-17 — Recipient-safe observability.** Logs, traces, retries, errors and live status SHALL not disclose secrets, private work/reviewer existence or restricted prompt derivatives to unauthorized model recipients or website/agent viewers. Usage and model-call status may be projected only within AOI/D-01 rights; sanitized errors must not falsely claim “no request sent” when the effect is unknown. *Basis: S01-17/26; AOI-04; R01-36; D-01.*

**IF12-18 — Startup and expiry.** One routine AEW startup unlock MAY prepare eligible gateways/adapters without separate routine credential prompts; startup state SHALL distinguish locked, preparing, ready functional, failed and expired components. Unlock is not G3, an effect grant, protected key custody or autonomous fallback approval. Route/key expiry shall invalidate only affected eligibility and preserve unrelated safe work. *Basis: S01-31; IF13-18; O01-15.*

**IF12-19 — Failure isolation and no silent substitution.** A missing upstream org gateway, unavailable provider, unqualified disclosure or denied paid class SHALL withhold its affected call and report the competent reason/currentness; IF-12 SHALL NOT silently switch to a personal provider, unrelated account, cheaper model, second SDK or disabled meter. Valid independent source-only, offline/no-charge and external operator-manual activity need not stop. *Basis: S01-16/24/27; R01-40; IF13-08/17.*

**IF12-20 — Independent selected-profile conformance.** The selected wire SHALL freeze original request/recipient/route identifiers, source-derived **and qualified whole-read-set** disclosure proof discriminants with complete scope and invalidation, qualified model/context receiving-ingress exposure producer/packet/recipient/source-version/ACK coverage and unknown semantics, pricing/free-tier chargeability basis, original intent→attempt→provider/billing mapping, streaming new-outbound-fragment admission, status and errors, cancellation/retry, gateway claim classes and version/epoch rules. Backend, provider adapter, independent CLI/site and attention consumers SHALL conform to distinct positive/adversarial fixtures; evidence from W0 schemas or synthetic cases alone does not qualify live selected host, G1–G4 or W1–W5. *Basis: S01-19/20/21/27/30; HEB-10; K01-10/15; P01-42; R01-41/42; IF13-20.*

## 3. Required original call decision order

The effects owner binds the work and original intent for **every real provider-bound request regardless of charge**; IF-13 determines the observed route; competent source owners qualify actual provider/account disclosure via per-fragment provenance or independently closed whole-read-set; O-01/R-01/IF-10 compare current policies and qualified admission/encumbrance; HEB/ADC/CNI decide any relevant effect; IF-12 performs one dispatch only after every applicable gate is satisfied. Separately, a protected receiving ingress (where actually established) records attributable context-packet delivery and makes bounded exposure observations available for K-01/P-01 changed-source attention; a provider send never fabricates that ingress receipt. There is no universal right for IF-12 to decide any missing gate is nonessential. On conflicting or unqualified sources, retain an attributable pending/held original operation and make only independent permissible progress.

## 4. Deployment profiles and deferred selections

- **WORK-ROCKY8-MTLS:** On-VM originator gateway required for the selected OpenCode/organization pairing; transport, routing and usage projection are functional; same-UID active-key non-bypass/custody and meter remain unqualified; org account ceiling can be independently evidenced *only for its exact account and window*. A proposed `WORK_FUNCTIONAL_COOPERATIVE` mode is **not** an adopted dispatch exception.
- **GENERAL-LOCAL:** Direct approved provider and no-charge fixtures without company PKI; an outside-account hard cap may qualify for its exact proved scope but never as the Ticket cap without independent accounting/admission. Permitted manual external contributions remain distinct.
- **Still open:** exact provider models, route error/stream protocol, actual upstream cap evidence, policy G3 and comparison, permitted work-source classes, agent-container proof and enabled release scope. No real model calls are authorized by this document.
