# AEW IF-10 — Resource Policy, Admissibility and Accounting Interface

**Candidate:** v0.2.1 · **2026-10-17** · **PROPOSED, NOT OPERATOR-ADOPTED**. **22 proposed obligations** (`IF10-01`–`IF10-22`). Companion: IF-12 v0.2 model delivery. IF-10 is the exact resource-interface slice subordinate to adopted R-01 v0.2.1; it is not a new policy, spending or protected host authority. **Scenario documents are unexecuted.**

## 1. Authority and relationship boundaries

Operator/O-01 and ADC govern original applicable policy and authority; R-01 owns resource admission/accounting semantics, with a protected writer/qualified meter required for hard-class positive claims; HEB/ADC own effect mediation; IF-13 describes effective capabilities; IF-12 does model delivery and provides attributed observations; external provider/org owns its own account caps and billing facts; AOI/D-01 own recipient-safe reads. CNI owns shared-effect interference admission; WIS owns work/revision/lineage identity. The selected work mTLS gateway is **cooperative observation only** on the same-UID VM, not an independently trusted ledger or billing custodian.

## 2. Proposed normative obligations

**IF10-01 — No second policy authority.** The interface SHALL materialize the selected R-01 resource-policy, admission, exposure, use, settlement and currentness facts without assigning a new grant, price, human authorization or cap-raising power to IF-10, a gateway, harness or website. A logical resource controller can share processes with other AEW parts only when actual protected claims are independently qualified. *Basis: R01-01/03/37; ADC-08; S01-13.*

**IF10-02 — Durable scope and inheritance.** Every resource policy SHALL bind accountable immutable policy-scope identity and revision, resource class, root and ancestor project/mission/provider/pool/window scopes, applicable work/revision, current operator issuance, effective interval and price/usage terms. A new generation, provider call, reattach, retry, nested advisor, agent or research subepisode does not reset an enclosing cap. *Basis: R01-02/05; R01-07; O01-08/09; WIS-01.*

**IF10-03 — Qualified resource and chargeability class.** For each actual operation, the competent operator/R-01 policy and exact qualified provider/account/model/route/price/terms/window producer SHALL distinguish `MANDATORY_HARD`, explicitly original operator-authorized `EXPRESS_COOPERATIVE`, **provider-bound `NO_CHARGE` established by competent current pricing/quota/overage evidence**, `LOCAL_NO_EXTERNAL_EFFECT_FIXTURE`, and `UNSUPPORTED_OR_UNKNOWN`. No-charge provider calls remain external effects with ADC/HEB/disclosure/route gates and any applicable request/rate/quota/concurrency policy. An adapter or worker `chargeable=false`, prepaid promotional balance, uncontrolled free-tier threshold, profile name or `FUNCTIONAL_ONLY` flag SHALL NOT certify `NO_CHARGE`; unknown possibility of overage follows the actually applicable financial policy and exposure handling, never automatic `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Source evidence SHALL establish positive zero charge for the intended maximum request and effective terms, or the gate is held/unknown as appropriate. For a finite shared free quota, credit or other consumable/conditional zero-price allowance, authenticated current terms and a recent remaining-balance observation alone SHALL NOT establish positive `NO_CHARGE`: concurrent original operations may exhaust the same allowance. The positive classification requires independently qualified exact provider/account/model/route/terms/window proof of (a) unconditional zero price for the bounded operation, (b) provider-enforced no-overage denial before any monetary charge for every eligible contested call, or (c) a contention-safe, qualified quota entitlement/reservation/fence for this original operation, effective across all competing routes and honored at dispatch. Where none is established, possible monetary exposure SHALL follow the actually authorized applicable paid class (including its required financial admission) or remain `HOLD`/unknown; no automatic downgrade to cooperative or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. These alternatives do not transfer price, quota, policy or admission authority to the worker, gateway or IF-10. *Basis: R01-03/09/40/43; S01-14/17/24; O01-08/09; HEB-01/03.*

**IF10-04 — Competent standing decision.** Cooperative paid class eligibility SHALL refer to an explicit original operator governance decision external to the measured agent lineage, with independently comparable current/revoked state, exact recipient/provider/account, work/policy scope, bounds and operator-accepted enforcement limitations. A local runtime file is a projection; absent/not comparable original source SHALL NOT be reinterpreted as issued permission. Any alternative work-compatibility path requires its own separately reviewed source-level authority and release selection. *Basis: S01-14; O01-08/09/15; AOI-07.c.*

**IF10-05 — Non-ambiguous bound meaning.** The selected contract SHALL distinguish `NOT_SPECIFIED`, explicitly `NO_AEW_NUMERIC_MAXIMUM` (only when competent policy actually permits it), `FINITE_MAXIMUM` (with unit/currency/period), unknown, and out-of-applicability bounds. Null/missing SHALL NEVER silently translate into unlimited authorized spending. Mandatory-hard limits SHALL have an enforceable finite bound; zero is a valid deny-all policy value for positive-priced effects, not malformed policy or positive remaining headroom. Any positive monetary admission SHALL demonstrate qualified positive remaining capacity for the selected operation's conservatively bounded worst-case cost; a stricter applicable parent bound governs. *Basis: R01-05/09/42; O01-09/18; S01-14.*

**IF10-06 — Class-specific qualification.** Every resource admission capability SHALL report the exact class/provider/account/source issuer, effective principal and route population, scope, independently qualified meter/writer/admission/non-bypass evidence, observation latency and currentness. Unproven class is `NOT_ESTABLISHED` even when usage estimates are available; no omnibus `hard_budget_supported` boolean qualifies another path. *Basis: R01-08/09/42/43; S01-02/09/13; IF13-06/07.*

**IF10-07 — Owner-derived original operation.** The competent admission/coordination owner SHALL derive original operation and equivalence from actual WIS work/revision, root grant/generation and effect subject. Client aliases, request headers, local model-side operation IDs and plugin-generated lineage do not create new charge capacity or protected attribution. Each attempt and provider charge preserves its mapping to this original intent. *Basis: R01-02/07/08; IF13-11; O01-06.*

**IF10-08 — Pre-effect applicable resource gate.** IF-10 SHALL return typed `MAY_EVALUATE_OTHER_GATES`, `HOLD`, `DENY`, `UNKNOWN_EFFECT`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with qualified/not-qualified source, scope, currentness and price-class basis. `NOT_APPLICABLE` applies only to truly out-of-scope resource effects, notably independently established local deterministic no-external-effect fixtures, **not** to every purportedly free external provider delivery; even source-qualified `NO_CHARGE` must pass applicable request/quota/concurrency policy and all nonresource IF12-11 gates. For finite shared free allowances, independently qualified **no-overage** enforcement or a contention-safe original-operation quota entitlement SHALL cover every eligible concurrent consumer and effective route; a common last-unit snapshot cannot grant two no-charge admissions. An exhausted hard-deny-only allowance may result in a denied provider attempt without billing, not a falsely fulfilled inference. Conditional free eligibility with possible bill-overage SHALL instead use the applicable actually authorized paid admission or `HOLD`. A zero mandatory-hard bound is a valid deny-all ceiling for a positive-priced call, **never** positive monetary headroom. `MAY_EVALUATE_OTHER_GATES` does not grant effect permission or guarantee reservation without a qualified source. Missing qualified provider pricing/possible overage and unsupported mandatory monetary worst case produce the applicable hold/unknown state, not a self-authorized free pass. *Basis: R01-03/07/09/40/43; S01-14/24; O01-08.*

**IF10-09 — Hard admission and atomic parent conservation.** For a supported hard class, the protected writer SHALL atomically/fenced compare current parent and child policies, qualified headroom, in-flight exposure and worst-case price and encumber before effect. Two concurrent children cannot both spend the same remaining headroom, including through separate gateway pools and detach/reattach. A local same-UID CAS or simulated concurrency is insufficient for a protected admission claim. *Basis: R01-05/06/07/39/43.*

**IF10-10 — Bound one paid request before sending.** Where an actual provider call cannot be reliably halted mid-flight, a hard-limit-positive reservation SHALL include an independently established enforceable worst-case charge at the selected model/pricing/maximum-request parameters and all ancestors. Configured token maximum alone does not establish a provider upper bound. Without comparable worst case, mark `RESOURCE_UNSUPPORTED_FOR_HARD_LIMIT` and withhold that hard-dependent dispatch; preserve mocks and independent permitted work. *Basis: R01-08/09/40; S01-14/24.*

**IF10-11 — Cooperative does not mean unaccountable.** A legitimately original approved cooperative class MAY produce attributed provisional requests, estimates and late billing observations under all other applicable gates. Such records SHALL conspicuously name who can alter them, qualified external account bound (if known), missing per-task enforcement, estimate method and source freshness, and SHALL NOT assert protected `RESERVED`, enforced Ticket cap or guaranteed remaining capacity. *Basis: R01-08/36/43; S01-11/13/14.*

**IF10-12 — External account cap exact scope.** A provider/org account-level cap SHALL be represented separately with provider/account identity, issuer, ceiling/unit/window, effective route population, account-side controls and independently confirmed status. Only that issuer-qualified scope MAY be described as externally bounded. A $100 organizational account cap is not proof of an AEW project/Ticket $10 hard cap or ownership of spend within the account. Unknown provider exclusions/route coverage means the asserted cap is `NOT_ESTABLISHED` for those routes. *Basis: R01-05/09/40/43; S01-13/14.*

**IF10-13 — Meter trust and non-bypass.** Meter evidence eligible for positive enforcement or downward settlement SHALL be produced and checked outside metered lineage reach, with complete route/non-bypass coverage, bound original operation, provider response/call, comparable usage and price, and protected results. An on-VM gateway with same-UID usable keys and alternate SDK access is cooperative only until actual adverse conformance proves otherwise; agent-side logs can conservatively *increase* suspicion, not establish reduced exposure. *Basis: R01-08/41/43; S01-09/11/13/18.*

**IF10-14 — Typed financial state.** `POLICY_OBSERVED`, `ADMISSION_REQUESTED`, `RESERVED_QUALIFIED`, `DISPATCH_ATTEMPTED`, `IN_FLIGHT`, `USAGE_OBSERVED_COOPERATIVE`, `UNPRICED_OR_UNKNOWN_EXPOSURE`, `PROVIDER_BILL_OBSERVED`, `SETTLED_QUALIFIED`, `RECONCILED` and `RELEASED_QUALIFIED` SHALL remain distinguishable in the selected wire or equivalent machine-enforced semantics. UI cost estimates shall not look like confirmed settlement or protected budget availability. *Basis: R01-05/08/09/36.*

**IF10-15 — Non-overlapping conservation.** A qualified reservation and corresponding settled consumption SHALL reconcile under the **same original operation** with no double charge and no premature free headroom. Qualified release requires comparable complete provider/account evidence or another competent justified closure; unknown external/late billing retains encumbrance conservatively where mandatory hard class requires it. *Basis: R01-05/08; HEB-08; R01-32.*

**IF10-16 — Revisions, corrections and event order.** A provider bill arriving after local timeout, Lead detach, restart or period rollover SHALL retain original intent, scope, price and correction history. An older usage estimate, outdated source epoch, missing webhook or client-time order cannot clear a newer qualified charge/encumbrance; duplicate settlement and ambiguous billing references are reconciled explicitly. *Basis: R01-08/31/32/39/40; IF13-13.*

**IF10-17 — Effect-time policy currentness.** Between policy check and actual model dispatch, any applicable parent-cap change, receipt of a newer operator revocation, provider/account/model switch, differing effective recipient, altered worst-case bound or changed grant shall force competent current comparison. A preflight policy receipt by itself is not a paid-call authorization. *Basis: R01-39/40; S01-22/24; O01-09/13.*

**IF10-18 — Provider outage and uncertainty.** Distinguish provider unreachable, quota exhausted, policy denied, ambiguous charge, attribution unresolved, cap-source unavailable and `UNKNOWN_EFFECT`. No cost-zero inference from gateway restart or failed stream, and no silent second paid call or provider substitution. Independent source-only/mock work continues where entitled. *Basis: R01-08/40/43; HEB-07/08; S01-16.*

**IF10-19 — Disclosure-safe accounting.** Resource denial and status views SHALL respect AOI/D-01 recipient entitlements and may not expose hidden verifier usage, private research activity, policy subjects or alternate capacity by amounts/counts/timing/side-channel distinctions. Operator aggregate estimates and worker-visible narrowed status may legitimately differ, but neither self-issues grants. *Basis: R01-36; AOI-04/06; S01-21.*

**IF10-20 — Same-UID proof limitation.** Where no outside-lineage protected issuer/writer, non-bypassable meter or effect mediation exists, protected admission, hard per-Ticket reservation, down-settlement and guaranteed resource capacity SHALL remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Independent provider-side cap may establish *its own exact scope only*; manual/external work is separately governed under HEB-02.k/l. A mode label cannot alter this. *Basis: R01-08/43; S01-11/14; HEB-02.*

**IF10-21 — Host and model adapter change.** A new SDK/plugin/agent-container/credential/route/provider may change the set of billable reachable effects; changed route inventory invalidates only affected metering/non-bypass claims and forces recomparison at the next effect. Functional profile selection may still be `EFFECTIVE` with `NOT_ESTABLISHED` controller qualification; it is not a new cap or original operator policy. *Basis: IF13-05/08/09/14; S01-09/22; R01-39.*

**IF10-22 — Selected resource wire and conformance.** Before independent implementation claims, selected IF-10 profile SHALL freeze original operator/policy/issuer/epoch/comparison, qualified unconditional/no-overage/fenced-free-entitlement proof across contention and route changes versus free-tier/overage terms, local fixture vs external-model resource discriminants, bound variants/null meaning, call/reservation/charge IDs, decimal/currency handling, price/currentness, non-bypass and effect-time policy, idempotency/settlement, recipient-safe refusals and machine-executable per-case oracles. Cross-field invalid combinations (`MANDATORY_HARD` zero/unknown bounds offered as positive headroom, external paid `NOT_APPLICABLE`, unqualified pricing as `NO_CHARGE`, contradictory admission/issuer states) SHALL fail independent semantic qualification even if a draft JSON shape parses. Actual qualified host/provider/website fixtures, not W0 schemas alone, establish claims. *Basis: R01-41/42/43; S01-19/20/27; O01-18; AOI/D-01.*

## 3. Admission result order and integration behavior

The competent operator policy source and inherited parent roster are compared first; unresolved or forbidden policies prevent permission (not all failures require evidence of a positive denial). Next assess actual resource class and independently qualified hard writer/meter/price/worst-case if mandatory; reserve atomically or hold. For an authorized cooperative path, compare the original external governance decision, scope and actual selected source/recipient, produce **only** a cooperative class gate; do not mint protected reservation or waive HEB/ADC. IF-12 and protected effect owner must recheck at delivery. Unknown in-flight/billing holds just the affected cap; no global offline mock shutdown.

2. If that cannot be demonstrated, selected release review must identify the specific adopted requirement blocking the route and propose an explicit narrow operator-adopted amendment **before** relaxing it, or retain externally operator-directed/manual model work with accurately attributed untrusted contributions.
