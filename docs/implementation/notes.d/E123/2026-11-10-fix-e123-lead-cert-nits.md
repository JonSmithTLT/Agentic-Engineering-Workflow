**The contained Lead's certificate provider, after the quick check's nits (`fix/e123-lead-cert-nits`).** This corrects the earlier note's line that a Lead under an `allow_weaker` fallback only says which providers it can't use.
- **No relay after `establish`:** a sandbox that can't be established under `allow_weaker` leaves no relay. A Lead whose only provider takes the certificate is refused there (`client_cert_no_relay`), as a worker is, before it is labelled weaker or entered in the register. A Lead with another provider runs weaker, and says once that it can't use the certificate provider.
- **Its models:** only profiles of the Lead's own harness (OpenCode) give the Lead a certificate provider's models. Another harness may name the same model differently.
- **No model, no relay:** a certificate provider no such profile pins a model of isn't relayed, and the Lead's start says what to pin. A Lead with no other provider is refused for it (`HARNESS_INCOMPATIBLE`, reason `client_cert_no_model`).
- **Left for the Lead's start-line row:** under `proxy_only` the start line says a certificate provider is relayed before the sandbox is known, as the key and login lines say theirs. A Lead with a key whose sandbox then falls back is told both.
