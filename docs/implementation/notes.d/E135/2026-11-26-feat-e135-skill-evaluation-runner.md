**The runner is built** (`eval/skill_eval/`; its [README](../../../../eval/skill_eval/README.md) walks through it). It runs the [skill evaluation guide](../../../skills/evaluation-guide.md)'s paired comparison for one skill AEW's install ships, on F19's instrument: a candidate's public cases become F19 cases that hold nothing of the oracle; the two arms are F19's `raw` (or, for a dry run, `scripted`) arm with a skill exposure, delivered by `aew.skills` under the projection's own skill rules; a preregistration is held to the paired shape, to a keyed model and to a budget of at most USD 10 before anything is registered; the schedule runs pair by pair under that budget and resumes from the ledger; and the report gives the row's three measures from the run records. Cost is the paired difference in usage, selection sets `loaded` beside each case's expected activation, and benefit is paired counts from a blind grader's file. Proved offline in `tests/unit/test_skill_eval.py` and against the fake OpenCode server in `tests/integration/test_skill_eval_opencode.py`. No model has run it and nothing was spent.

**What remains** on this row is the qualification itself, the release gate before anything beyond the personal alpha: the operator's paid runs, for every skill served. Before the first one:

- **A model-free live check on the pinned OpenCode** that the `skill` tool lists exactly an arm's exposed ids under the session rules the arm sends. No OpenCode binary was at hand when the runner was built, and the fake server applies no skill rules.
- **The choices the runner leaves to the plan** (the README's "What the runner leaves open"): who grades and by what rubric, the model and its effort, the sample and the decision rule, and what the arm is told.
- **Private cases.** The public cases are development material, so every run on them is a pilot. F19 refuses a held-out case on a model arm until its evaluator/arm-host split is finished, and F19 is frozen.
- **The agent.** The model arm is OpenCode's own `build` agent with the skill exposed, not a worker under an AEW role card; that needs F19's `aew` arm, which is not built.

The earlier result on this row's Notes (no detectable benefit on cases that did not discriminate) is not re-examined here.
