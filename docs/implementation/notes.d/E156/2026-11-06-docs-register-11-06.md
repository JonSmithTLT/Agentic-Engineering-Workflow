- A worker launch on the relay route whose provider would get no key is refused before anything is committed (`provider_key_missing`), by the engine and again by the supervisor, from one pure predicate (`netns.provider_key_missing`).
- Every Lead's configuration denies `provider.use` for `opencode` and `opencode-go`. The capability probe checks `experimental` and `experimental.policies`.
- A contained Lead with no provider relayed is refused at start.
- The egress proxy records refused CONNECT targets in the run's record only (`egress_refused`, beside the label), with one fixed console-sync note.
- Doctor gains a `provider-key` line, and `aew init` a first-use line. `aew init --model opencode/...` now writes opencode's key variable.
- The live lanes have no default model. ADR-0009 has an amendment of 2026-11-06.
