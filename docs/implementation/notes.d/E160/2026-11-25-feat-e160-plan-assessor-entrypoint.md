**Built, slice 2a of slice 2 (`feat/e160-plan-assessor-entrypoint`): the assessor entrypoint's engine side, its pack and the anti-shopping refusal.** `aew plan assess <W>` dispatches a plan assessor for a Ticket's or a parent's accepted plan revision: a reviewer invocation at scope `plan_assessment` (the `plan.assess` dispatch entrypoint, with its guards `assess.unit`, `assess.card`, `assess.once` and `git.drivers`), with its own read-only observation of the authoritative source, pinned to the revision it assesses. Its card is the new built-in `plan_assessor` (it extends `reviewer` and is narrowed to `submit.plan_assessment` and `context.read`), or a named card of that archetype that lists `plan_assessment` among its outputs. The reviewer archetype's grant now includes `submit.plan_assessment`, but `evidence.kinds_for` keeps the kind to the assessor's scope alone. A reviewer of a change, at any other scope, is refused with `not_a_plan_assessment_dispatch`, and an assessor submits nothing else (`plan_assessment_dispatch`). A run's expected kinds, its bridge operations and its pack's allowed operations follow the same function (`evidence.submit_operations`), so a reviewer of a change is never offered the assessment. Nothing ingests a plan assessment: a finished assessor's run says whether its assessment counts, and prints the `aew invoke cancel` that ends the invocation and retires its observation and credential. An assessment is bound by `plan_assessment_binding`, and it is refused once the accepted plan is another revision than the one its assessor was pinned to (`plan_revision_changed`). The assessor's pack holds the requirement, the request as recorded (each ancestor's record, body and acceptance: AEW keeps no other preserved request), the accepted plan, an assessment scope, the codebase map where maps are on, and the report format and allowed values from the evidence schema. It holds no consumed inputs, so no planner reasoning, and no open findings of a change. **Anti-shopping:** once an assessment of the accepted revision counts (`counting_assessment`: the first recorded, ruling 4 as built), a further `aew plan assess` of that revision is refused (`ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`, reason `plan_revision_assessed`), naming the counting record and saying that a new revision is assessed afresh. This refusal depends on ruling 4 as built. The command takes the Lead credential and, until its typed tool lands, sits in `lead_broker.TYPED_PENDING` (part of `OPERATOR_ONLY`): a Lead session's broker refuses it and says why, and the operator runs it at their own terminal. The typed tool decides its `lead_disposition` (the surface map's standing rule). (2) Assessor shopping beyond what the refusal holds, each left unrefused beside ruling 4 (#581 review, F3): by cancel, where a Lead cancels an assessor before it submits and dispatches another; by re-proposal, where the identical plan text proposed and accepted as a new revision is assessed afresh, since the refusal binds a revision and its sha256, not the text (the unchanged-plan case ruling 4 names); and by concurrency, where two assessors of one revision are both admitted and the first recorded counts.
