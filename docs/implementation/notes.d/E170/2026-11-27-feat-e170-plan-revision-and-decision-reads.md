**Built, a further slice of the CLI half (`feat/e170-plan-revision-and-decision-reads`): `aew record body` reads a plan revision and a decision record.** The note of 2026-11-26 asked the operator whether "any record" includes them. **The operator's answer, 2026-11-27:** "I personally would want to be able to read revisions and decisions and as an operator that would be useful and alead being able to view decision records and a specific plan revision would be valuable".

- **A plan revision** has no id of its own, so it is named by its unit and its number: `aew record body <work-id> --plan <N>`, the number `aew plan accept --revision` takes and `aew work show` lists. It reads the file the unit's `plans` entry names and holds it to the hash that entry pins (what `plan propose` wrote and `plan accept` compares), for a current unit from control state and for an archived one from its bundle (`Archive.archived_unit`, as `history show` finds it). `--json` gives `plan_revision`, `status`, `accepted` (whether it is the unit's accepted plan) and `supersedes`. Refusals: `NOT_FOUND` with the new reason `unknown_revision` (its details list the unit's revisions), `unknown_record` for an id that is no work unit; `INTEGRITY_ERROR` with `not_pinned`, `unreadable` or `not_text`, as for the other records.
- **A decision record** (`D-n`, `Kernel.new_decision`, `.aew/decisions/<id>.md`) is pinned by nothing in control state: its pin is the hash the transition that wrote it recorded in its writes, in the hash-chained transition log, which the read walks back from the committed head (`outbox.newest_transitions`, reading only the chain and each record's writes, never its events), to the start of the log before it refuses one, whatever the decision's own time says, since a clock corrected after it was recorded dates later commits before it. A decision whose content is not that hash, or that no transition wrote (planted), is refused `INTEGRITY_ERROR` `not_pinned`; one whose number was issued (within the decision counter) and whose file is gone, `unreadable`; a damaged log, `log_damaged`. A decision's body is the engine's account of what it decided, with the values it was given, and is printed as written. Most decisions have none and hold everything in their fields, and the Lead has no dashboard, so such a decision answers with its record itself, its fields in the file's own order and format, under the same pin, escaping and integrity reasons (the coordinator's change to this slice, 2026-11-27, to keep to the ruling's "a lead being able to view decision records"); `no_body` stays only for kinds with nothing to print. `--json` adds `decision_type` and `summary`, and for a decision without a body `record` (the text) and `fields` (parsed).
- **The Lead** runs every form in its own shell, as before (`lead_broker.CONTAINED_LOCAL`; the broker relays only commands that take the Lead credential, and `.aew` outside `.aew/local` is visible read-only in the sandbox), so nothing was added to the broker and no typed tool was added. The surface map's entry names the new probes, forms and reasons; its sample gains a second Ticket whose dependency change is a decision with a body (`D-0003`), and probes the plan's acceptance (`D-0002`), a decision without one.

**Limits:** a decision recorded before a project's transition log began (an engine older than ADR-0012) has no pin, so it is not printed: it is refused `INTEGRITY_ERROR` `before_log`, without the text that calls it damage, when the project has no log at all (tested), or when the log began after the project did, its id is one AEW issued (exactly `D-nnnn`, within the decision counter) and the time it records is before the log's first transition (the message then names that revision and its time; no fixture reaches this second case, since a test log always begins at the project's start). Anything else that no transition wrote is `not_pinned`, so a planted decision above the counter cannot pass for an old one by claiming an early time (tested). An issued decision whose file is gone is `unreadable`; `D-1`, `D-0` and `D-0000` are not issued ids and stay `unknown_record`. The search reads the log from its head back to the transition that wrote the decision, so an old decision costs a longer walk, and a refusal reads the whole log.
