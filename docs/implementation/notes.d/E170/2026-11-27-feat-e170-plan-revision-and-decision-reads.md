**Built, a further slice of the CLI half (`feat/e170-plan-revision-and-decision-reads`): `aew record body` reads a plan revision and a decision record.** The note of 2026-11-26 asked the operator whether "any record" includes them. **The operator's answer, 2026-11-27:** "I personally would want to be able to read revisions and decisions and as an operator that would be useful and alead being able to view decision records and a specific plan revision would be valuable".

- **A plan revision** has no id of its own, so it is named by its unit and its number: `aew record body <work-id> --plan <N>`, the number `aew plan accept --revision` takes and `aew work show` lists. It reads the file the unit's `plans` entry names and holds it to the hash that entry pins (what `plan propose` wrote and `plan accept` compares), for a current unit from control state and for an archived one from its bundle (`Archive.archived_unit`, as `history show` finds it). `--json` gives `plan_revision`, `status`, `accepted` (whether it is the unit's accepted plan) and `supersedes`. Refusals: `NOT_FOUND` with the new reason `unknown_revision` (its details list the unit's revisions), `unknown_record` for an id that is no work unit; `INTEGRITY_ERROR` with `not_pinned`, `unreadable` or `not_text`, as for the other records.
- **A decision record** (`D-n`, `Kernel.new_decision`, `.aew/decisions/<id>.md`) is pinned by nothing in control state: its pin is the hash the transition that wrote it recorded in its writes, in the hash-chained transition log, which the read walks back from the committed head (`outbox.newest_transitions`) as far as a day before the decision's own time. A decision whose content is not that hash, or that no transition wrote (planted), is refused `INTEGRITY_ERROR` `not_pinned`. A decision's body is the engine's account of what it decided, with the values it was given; most decisions have none and hold everything in their fields, so they are refused `NOT_FOUND` `no_body`, which names the dashboard's knowledge view and the record's file. `--json` adds `decision_type` and `summary`.
- **The Lead** runs every form in its own shell, as before (`lead_broker.CONTAINED_LOCAL`; the broker relays only commands that take the Lead credential, and `.aew` outside `.aew/local` is visible read-only in the sandbox), so nothing was added to the broker and no typed tool was added. The surface map's entry names the new probes, forms and reasons; its sample gains a second Ticket whose dependency change is a decision with a body (`D-0003`).

**Limits:** a decision recorded before a project's transition log began (an engine older than ADR-0012) has no pin and is refused `not_pinned`, whose message says the log's first revision; no fixture reaches that case. The decision search reads the log back to about a day before the decision, so an old decision costs a longer walk.
