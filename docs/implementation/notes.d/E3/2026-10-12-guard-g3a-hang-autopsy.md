The hang-and-flake diagnosis plan's first slice (G3a, PR #217) builds the autopsy, the session deadline and the nested bounds. Its review (2026-10-12) left three records for this row, which holds the CI's intermittent failures until G3c's flake register exists. No register row tracks G3 itself yet; these move to it, or to that register, when one exists.

- **Not reproduced (the flake register's first entry when G3c lands).** `tests/integration/test_hang_autopsy.py::test_the_deadline_ends_only_the_sessions_own_processes[xdist]` failed once while it was built. It is recorded as failed once, output lost. The suspected cause is a slow controller whose deadline collect outlasted the 3 s test margins, so the sentinel ended it. Since then:
  - its margins are 20 s and 10 s, and its assertions print the session's kept files;
  - `test_a_controller_slower_than_its_margins_is_ended_by_the_sentinel_and_says_so` makes that path deterministic;
  - the `controller-ended` and `session-finished` markers record the time since the deadline.

  More than 20 reruns and the review's 32 runs passed. Owner: G3c.
- **Tests that leave processes running.** With `tmp_path_retention_policy = "failed"`, a later test reused a passing test's directory. A process the earlier test left running then wrote into it, and `test_every_invocation_ending_commit_seals_its_thread[…]` (`test_coordination_lifecycle.py`) broke its transition hash chain. That family is the first known case. The retention stays pytest's default. G3a adds a report-only scan, `leaked-processes.<worker>.txt` in the autopsy directory: at each module's end and at session end, it lists processes whose command line or working directory lies in the base temporary directory, by their test's directory. Enforcement, with a shrink-only allow-list, is G3b's.
- **A residual risk in the Windows faulthandler.** On CPython 3.13.1, faulthandler's repeating dump, which helper children run through `childdiag`, crashed a child process in reproduction. Two causes are fixed: a dump at interpreter exit, and a rotation in phase with the dumps. One is not: a dump while another thread runs Python code. Its exposure is highest in the tests that dump every 1 to 2 s (`test_hang_autopsy`, `test_hang_autopsy_units`, the #213 reconstruction). A child's native crash now names itself in the failure: its `fatal-<pid>.txt`, through `autopsy.native_crashes`.
