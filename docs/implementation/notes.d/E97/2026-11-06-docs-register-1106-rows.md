**The rate unit** (the designer's decision of 2026-11-06, rate limits that count cached tokens per request, ruling 1): a provider profile declares its `rate_basis`, the usage ledger's counters its limiter counts (`input`, `cache_read`, `cache_write`, `output`), and the limit's window. When the basis is undeclared, every counter counts, the conservative reading. E97's meter and I-6's ceilings use that unit and its period (`IF10-05`). AEW sets no number: the operator supplies the provider's limit, and the policy names its source. A provider's limiter can count cache reads per request, so a request costs its whole context against the limit, cached or not (`AEW-FR-10.c`, `AEW-FR-26.b`, `AEW-FR-26.c`).
