**E6a, the integration stages and the custodian's worker** (2026-10-10; [ADR-0004](../../adr/0004-controlled-integration.md),
amendment 2026-10-10; plan v3 §2.3). `ticket_prepare` and `integration_publish` (with `verification_evidence`) are
built, with VALIDATE_ONLY's `VALIDATED` entry; the lease's custodian holds a custody credential and runs in a custody
worker (`engine/custody_ops.py`, `aew integrate custody`), committing through `Kernel.custody_txn` only; a clean
validation releases the lease to `VALIDATED`, and a later publish takes a fresh lease. The normal advertised surface
is 11,694 bytes, 15 tools. Tests: `tests/integration/test_integration_stages.py`. Next: E6b, PIC.
**Deviations from plan v3, recorded (PR #200 review):** `integration_publish` also expands to the integration
verifier's dispatch and launch, planned only on a moved head in verifier mode, because §2.3 T3 says the stage launches
the rebuilt candidate's verifier (the E4 table lists only the ingest and the publish). No requirements-ledger rows
land with the ADR-0004 amendment: ADR-0004 predates the ledger (`FIRST_LEDGER_ADR = 12`) and is tracked here. The Lead
broker needs no edit: its bridge runs only Lead-authenticated commands, so `aew integrate custody` (which takes no Lead
credential) is never relayed, and it refuses to run where a Lead session's environment is present.
