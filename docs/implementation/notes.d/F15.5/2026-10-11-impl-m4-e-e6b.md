**Built (M4-E E6b, the publication grants; [plan v3](../../m4-e-plan-v3.md) §2.2, §2.3;
[ADR-0004](../../adr/0004-controlled-integration.md), amendment 2026-10-11).** `PUBLISH_IF_CLEAN`'s envelope is the
operator's: `aew operator grant-publication --manifest <sealed run manifest> --task <task file> [--for] [--max]` and
`aew operator revoke-publication` go through the operator endpoint (new operations `grant_publication` and
`revoke_publication`, each with the peer check and the challenge on its console, the manifest's and the task's hashes
shown), and the engine commits only for the `OperatorPrincipal` the endpoint constructs. The Lead broker refuses both
in a Lead session (`lead_broker.OPERATOR_ENDPOINT`), and no typed tool grants. The Lead only requests, with
`ticket_prepare(publish_if_clean: true)`, which grants nothing; the lease's custodian exercises it in its checks-mode
validation commit, never the steering loop (A1 §5), so no catalog tool becomes auto-runnable and
`test_the_live_catalog_meets_what_the_loop_relies_on` is unchanged. Every record is `guarantee: dev`.

The envelope (`engine/publication_ops.py`) binds the sealed manifest's SHA-256 (re-verified at every exercise), the
task input's hash (checked against `--task` at grant), the granting Lead generation, the authoritative ref, an expiry,
a publication maximum, the legality digest and the policy pins. Its duration and maximum default to the manifest's
`envelope_duration_s` and `max_publications` (ADR-0018 D4: "the ones the ceremony grants") and may only narrow them,
never past 72 h. Control state keeps it as `authorization`; its record is `records/authorizations/<PA-n>.yaml`. A
generation change, expiry, a manifest mismatch or exhaustion ends it in the next transaction (a finalizer), with the
reason, which M4-E E7d turns into `envelope_ended`. `status` shows it (`publication_envelope`).
