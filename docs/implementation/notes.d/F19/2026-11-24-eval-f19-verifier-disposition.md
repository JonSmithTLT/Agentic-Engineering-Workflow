**Verifier-disposition rehearsal (2026-11-24; the first deliverable of the operator-supplied audit of public main at `14712ee8`, its §1):** `eval/experiments/verifier-disposition/` preregisters `verifier-disposition-offline-1` (`purpose: qualification`, budget 0). Its two scripted arms differ only in skill exposure: the shipped verifier card with `acceptance-verification` withheld, or the card unchanged. The cases are three public candidates for the M3 T5 Ticket: the reference, the seeded defect, and a repair whose shares add up but put the leftover cents on the last shares. A public offline adapter turns a verifier's first emitted verification into a disposition. `accept` is exactly what the verification gate passes. It records separately each claim its observed checks do not support, any edit to the candidate, and cost (null when unmeasured). A run whose delivered exposure is not its arm's, or whose record is bound to another snapshot, is invalid. An evaluator-only grader grades blinded observations as correct accept, justified rework, false accept or false rework, and the preregistration commits to its expected decisions. The calibration re-derives those decisions from the M3 T5 hidden checks. The grader rejects a plausible false accept, a plausible false rework and a fabricated accept. Proved in `tests/unit/test_eval_verifier_disposition.py` and `tests/integration/test_eval_verifier_disposition_rehearsal.py`. The scripted verifier ignores the skill, so this qualifies delivery and grading only. Effectiveness evidence needs separately approved model attempts and fresh private variants (`aew-private/eval/`). It also needs a verifier dispatched through AEW and launched by the adapter, with its skill loads read from the session; none of these is built here. The instrument itself (`eval/aew_eval/`) is unchanged.
