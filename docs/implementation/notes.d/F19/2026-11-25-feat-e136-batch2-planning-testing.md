**The verifier-disposition experiment's arms, from E136's batch 2 (review of #574, m1).** E136's second batch gives the `verifier` card `optional_skills` (`test-design` and `mechanical-enumeration`). The experiment's adapter replaces only the card's `skills:`, so from that batch both arms are given the card's `optional_skills` too: the `inaccessible` arm is offered and delivered those two, and the `exposed` arm those two and `acceptance-verification`. The arms still differ by exactly `acceptance-verification`, which is what `plan.yaml` states ("the verifier card with acceptance-verification withheld"), and the offline tests check that (`tests/unit/test_eval_verifier_disposition.py`, `tests/integration/test_eval_verifier_disposition_rehearsal.py`). The experiment README's "none: nothing is copied" for the withheld arm predates that batch; the frozen material under `eval/` is left as it is. The scripted arms load no skill, so no recorded result changes; a model-run arm would see the two offered skills in both arms alike.
