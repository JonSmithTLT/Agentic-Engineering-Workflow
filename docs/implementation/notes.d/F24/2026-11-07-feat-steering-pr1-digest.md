**The Codex adapter's steering work, recorded by steering PR 1** (the harness capability declarations, `aew.harness.harness_capabilities.CODEX`, rendered in `docs/reference/harness-capabilities.md`). A live probe of the Codex 0.160.0 app-server and the 0.162.1 standalone app-server, against a loopback stub with no model called, found two gaps this row holds. (1) `queue_until_turn_end`: `turn/start` on a busy thread is steered into the running turn and there is no native queue, so the adapter holds a wake until `thread/status/changed` reports idle. (2) `attach_to_operator_session`: AEW hosts the Lead's app-server (`--listen unix://<path>` or a loopback `ws://` with a bearer token) and the operator starts the TUI with `codex --remote <endpoint>`; a TUI with its own embedded server cannot be reached, because a second process on the same home is refused as "already has an active writer". Also for the adapter: use `thread/inject_items` (snake case) with a developer-role message to mark a wake as not the operator's, keeping the text header; and its first step re-checks `thread/resume` with history persistence on, `wss://` with `--remote-auth-token-env`, whether the TUI shows a turn another client started, and the pushed `read_progress` and `read_usage` items. When the adapter lands it declares its own column and `CODEX` goes.
