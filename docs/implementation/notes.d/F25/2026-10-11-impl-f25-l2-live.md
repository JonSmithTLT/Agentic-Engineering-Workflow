**M4-H live usage, slice L2 built** (2026-10-10): a supervised run publishes its spend while it runs. The OpenCode
adapter creates the run's meter first and writes `prompt_state: unsent` to `usage.live.json` before the server starts
(a failed write refuses the launch); the meter then polls every second on its own thread and client, in every turn
state; `posting` and `posted` bracket the first prompt; a turn's end is sampled at once; `terminate` takes a fresh
final sample in at most 4 s all told, never waiting for a poll in flight (and no adapter call waits on the meter's
requests); a meter failure never ends or delays the run; the meter is the file's only writer, and a live write wakes
no waiter (`runlog.write_live`, `read_live`; [harness conformance](../../harness-conformance.md) §5.2; ADR-0009's
amendment of 2026-10-10). Tests: `tests/unit/test_usage_live_run.py`, `tests/integration/test_usage_live_runs.py`,
and `test_the_agent_cannot_write_its_live_usage` (Linux containment). L3 (the measured total and preflight) and L4
(the evaluator's reader) follow.
