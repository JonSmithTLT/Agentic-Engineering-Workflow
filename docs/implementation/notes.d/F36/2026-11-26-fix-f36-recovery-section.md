**What this delivers.** When an implementer, or the executor of a non-mutating Ticket (investigator, researcher,
planner), is dispatched in the place of one that ended without completing (the unit's latest earlier invocation of the
same role and scope, with a status other than active or completed), AEW assembles a "Recovery reference" section in
its pack, after every section its work depends on. It is labelled as reference data: never an instruction, authority
or Knowledge. AEW assembles it; the reason and the paths it quotes are recorded text that others wrote.
- **Each fact from its owner, read at dispatch:** the predecessor's id, role, scope and status (its invocation); the
  recorded reason its credential ended (its token record); the ids and kinds of the evidence it produced (the evidence
  store), never a review, a verification or a plan assessment; and, for an implementer, the workspace's paths that
  differ from its base, each marked staged, deleted or untracked (`fingerprint.workspace_paths`).
- **The recorded reason** now holds why an invocation was ended, where it held the status alone: the Lead's reason
  for `invoke cancel` and for `work redispatch`, and the engine's for a released workspace (a replan). It is recorded
  as one line of at most 300 characters with the cut counted. The same record is what the ended worker's next call is
  refused with, the `credential.revoked` event, its run's stop reason and the dashboard's authority line.
- **Bounded, and a cut is said:** 30 paths, 20 evidence ids, and 160 characters of a reason or a path as the pack
  prints it, each cut with its count ("and N more"). A reason and a path are each one JSON-quoted line. Under a
  context budget the section is kept whole or omitted whole, and named in the budget notice.
- **Pinned on the invocation** (`recovery`, and a `recovery:<predecessor>` entry in the pack's sources), so a
  regenerated pack is the dispatched one whatever the workspace does next.
- **Proof stays with its snapshot:** the section lists an evidence id and kind, never a result, and says before the
  ids that none of it is proof for the replacement's snapshot.
- **Independence: no evaluator gets the section.** A reviewer, a verifier and a plan assessor, at every scope, are
  never told of an earlier invocation, whether an implementer or another evaluator, and their packs are unchanged
  (the review of pull request #614: a finished plan assessor is ended by `invoke cancel`, and a reviewer's verdict
  can be recorded and never ingested, so neither status says the evaluator did not finish). **Widening the section
  to evaluators is the operator's decision, left open.**
- A first invocation, and one that follows completed work (a rework after a review), gets no section, and its pack
  and its record are unchanged.

It claims no effect on a worker. How much it helps is what Experiment C of the ProjectArchitect research measures
(the row E389 that pull request #604 adds; this section is C1's treatment there, its candidate delta D1).
