**G3-C: the owner consumes.** At the O-01 carrier's admission of a protected member, the owner computes G3's standing,
per request and never cached. When it fails, or the carrier has no project or the member no proof, the member is held,
`ORIGIN_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, with nothing allocated and consume not called. When it holds, the owner consumes once with the
digest of the set it received: `CONSUMED` is admitted, a refusal is rejected through the sealed table, and a service
that gives no result holds the member (G3-11). An admitted member carries its operation, with the effect `NOT_OBSERVED`
and no evidence ref, since no owner here performs a protected action; that is the next pull request's work for its one
action. A successor member with no session is also held now. Limit: consume runs inside the carrier's ledger
transaction, so a slow service holds the ledger for at most its bound, and a transaction that fails after consume leaves
the member used, which fails closed.
