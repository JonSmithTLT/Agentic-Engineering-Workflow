# Authoring and evaluation notes

Evaluator-only; exclude the entire evals directory from worker context.

## Authority and interfaces

Assignment supplies scope/responsibility, context supplies current facts and capabilities supply permitted operations. The skill supplies technique, not dispatch, publication, installation, credentials, spending or workflow-transition authority. Preserve existing evidence/report formats.

## Evidence status and hypothesis

Candidate 0.1.0 has not had a worker pilot. Direct worker evidence is weaker: this is primarily a preventive research capability plus transferred measurement practices. Hypothesis: the narrowly triggered procedure reduces the failures in the [rubric](rubric.md) without excessive activation, unsupported claims or context/tool cost. See [source rationale](sources.md).

## Evaluation

[Public cases](cases.yaml) are development material, not unseen holdouts. Before adoption freeze private tasks, all runtime resource digests, worker profile, environment and budget. Compare the same worker with/without this skill; include the relevant existing skill as a comparator for overlapping tasks. Keep other inputs constant. Separate natural activation from forced loading and measure composition cost.

Predeclare outcome criteria and acceptable cost/regression margins with the evaluation owner. Record correctness, unsupported claims, missed obligations, activation errors, tokens/tools/time and unresolved outcomes. Fabricated evidence or authority violations veto adoption. Structure validation does not establish effectiveness; a successful pilot grants no production authority.
