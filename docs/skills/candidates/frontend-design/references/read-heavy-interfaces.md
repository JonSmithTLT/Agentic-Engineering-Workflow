# Read-heavy interfaces

Read for inspectors, settings views and dashboards whose source, scope or observation can change. Follow maintained API, cache and lifecycle contracts; locate their owners before inventing a screen-local policy.

- **Meaning:** Preserve configured, effective and observed values as distinct concepts. Keep null, absent, withheld, unknown and failed states separate where the host defines them. Retain source identity, observation environment/time and applicable scope. Do not imply a live read or applied configuration from a stored snapshot.
- **Read boundaries:** Identify what starts a read, including hidden panels, prefetch, disclosure and subscriptions. A concealed control does not prove no read occurs. Apply the host's authorization/disclosure/cache policy to actual effects, not just visible UI; do not universally ban prefetch where the contract permits it.
- **Retirement:** Locate the owner of cancellation and scope lifetime. After awaited work, recheck retirement/current identity before another read or publishing a result. A cancellation request alone is insufficient when the source can ignore it. Already-started work may finish; its completion must not resurrect a retired scope or trigger prohibited follow-up work.
- **Replacement:** When a source is replaced, synchronously exclude its predecessor's result from the newly active source's display. Tag results with source/generation identity or use an equivalent host-owned mechanism. Do not wait for a later effect or replacement response to remove misleading attribution. Same-source refresh retention is a separate policy: retain stale data only as the contract permits and label it accordingly.
- **Evidence:** Exercise replacement while old work is pending, late success and late failure, retirement between reads, and disclosure/navigation transitions. Test a source that ignores cancellation. Verify both displayed state and prohibited read effects through deterministic seams; screenshots alone cannot establish lifecycle behavior.

Keep fixtures semantically coherent: changing an identifier also changes dependent locators/references, within actual bounds. Use the maintained contract or validated examples; a shape sketch alone is not a complete validator specification. Label deliberately invalid fixtures and the failure they target. Fixture validation does not create new product acceptance policy.
